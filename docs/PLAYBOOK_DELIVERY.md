# From the playbook to a defensible demo

The [18-step playbook](../curling-cv-playbook.html) describes two different projects that meet at a board-state record. A model evaluated on official CurlR states does not validate the video system that would supply those states. The [left/right contract](LEFT_RIGHT_REQUIREMENTS.md) takes priority when the two plans compete for time.

## Next independent work

**Direction-stratified event scoring (declared during source-only annotation, before validation inference).** Report one row per observed departure panel (left, right, unknown) and one pooled row, for each recording. Panel here means the broadcast panel showing the delivery end, not team colour or odd/even end. Keep source-observed direction separate from inferred direction. A hidden release with an observable arrival is not the same as a throw not shown at all.

- Match deliveries to predictions one-to-one on the common source timeline **before** grouping by side. Use comparable timestamp semantics: a predicted line crossing is not a release. Freeze the matching procedure, including uncertain time brackets and duplicates, before seeing validation predictions.
- Compute recall by reference direction (matched / eligible reference throws) and precision by predicted direction (matched / predictions). A wrong-side match remains an event match; report its direction error separately in a reference-by-predicted direction table. Keep unmatched predictions, missed references and unknown directions in the counts. A zero denominator means not estimable, not zero.
- Pool by summing counts, not by averaging side percentages. Show raw denominators and unmatched counts; a pooled score describes the evaluated footage, not transfer to all broadcasts.
- Annotate visibility from the source, separately from whether the model succeeded. Retain timestampable deliveries supported elsewhere in the footage even if chairs hide the release. Report release-visible vs release-occluded results where labels allow. List suspected/unlocalizable deliveries separately; do not invent release times, assume a full 16 as ground truth, or quietly count unreviewed footage as negatives. Event detection and observable trajectory coverage are separate outcomes.
