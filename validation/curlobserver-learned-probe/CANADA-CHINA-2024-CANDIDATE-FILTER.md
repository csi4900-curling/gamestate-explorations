# Canada–China 2024: candidate-centric receiving-motion filter

Development audit, 8 October 2026. The [frozen first corridor candidate set](../throw-event-benchmark/canada-china-2024/frozen-first-corridor.json) contains 21 passage candidates in the fixed 1650–2760 s interval. Unlike the earlier window-conditioned motion audit, this scan starts **only from each candidate's crossing time, colour, and direction**. It does not read the source-only delivery windows or their labels during inference. The windows are opened only by the separate scorer at the end.

The [candidate-centric runner](../../prototype/scripts/audit_candidate_centric_receiving_motion.py) checks the expected receiving view for up to 12 s after each passage, with the existing 1080p calibration, a 160 px development registration limit, and an observed far-to-house stone-motion path. The 1 Hz [first diagnostic](canada-china-2024-candidate-centric-motion-frozen-first-development.json) found 13 unique paths, three ambiguous paths, and five candidates without a path. One real, short left-view transit was missed at that sampling rate.

The same scan at 0.5 s for **all 21 candidates** produced [18 unique paths and three without a path](canada-china-2024-candidate-centric-motion-frozen-first-halfsecond-development.json). It recovered the short real transit, but also gave D11—a previously reviewed false passage—a smooth five-point red path. The [marked frames](canada-china-2024-D11-false-support-development.jpg) show that path is the red maple leaf on a moving player's white jersey. This is a direct counterexample to accepting smooth, calibrated motion as stone identity. D03, D05, and D23 had no supported path at either sampling rate.

An existing [frozen patch-appearance model](cap-patch-crossmatch-v1/model-excluding-china-dev.npz), trained on small exposed 2022 Slovakia and Switzerland crop sets, scored the first, middle, and last point of each selected path. The [appearance audit](canada-china-2024-candidate-motion-patch-appearance-development.json) gives D11 three negative patch scores (−0.31, −0.22, −0.20); every real selected path has at least one positive sampled score. A [two-cue screen](canada-china-2024-candidate-motion-patch-screen-development.json) requiring a unique motion path and one positive patch retains 17/21 passages. When scored afterward against the exposed, coarse source-only windows, its [in-sample result](canada-china-2024-candidate-motion-patch-score-development.json) is **17 TP / 0 FP / 1 FN**, precision 1.00, recall 0.944, F1 0.971. C01 remains missed because the original passage detector never proposed it; a filter cannot recover an absent candidate.

**This is not a generalization result.** The candidate set, four false flags, camera geometry, sampling rate, motion rule, and final one-of-three appearance combination were all exposed or selected during work on this match. The patch model predates this 2024 audit, but its source crops are tiny and agent-labelled. The scorer's broad windows do not measure release-time accuracy or certify complete-video exhaustiveness. A fair next test must freeze this two-cue rule, run it without edits on another recording's candidates and high-resolution source, and use independent source labels when available. The reserved unseen end remains untouched.

The [fixed-rule Canada–Estonia transfer](CANADA-ESTONIA-2024-MOTION-TRANSFER-RESULTS.md) subsequently retained only 6 of 17 coarse windows (recall 0.353). Later Estonia repairs are in-sample development and do not change that transfer result.
