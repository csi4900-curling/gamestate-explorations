The frozen first-pass runs and reserved unseen end were not changed.

In both transfer recordings, none of the added points turned a previously negative origin window into a positive one. The image-change rule is therefore useful for generating **alternative segmentations**, but the evidence does not support replacing the conservative cut timeline globally.

Each run has a local cut-effect audit, ledger comparison and multiscale uncertainty handoff in its `*-edit-augmented-development` directory. The official shot-order/count references lack source-video timestamps; no row above measures physical throw precision, recall or F1. The game-state and simulation stages still require verified event identity, complete settled boards, calibrated trajectories and release/settle times.

## Visual-only appearance check

The same frozen cap-patch model was applied at 5 Hz to **all visual-only candidates** in each augmented ledger, without modifying any event decisions. A sustained cap track with a median stone-like patch score appears in 9/21 Korea–China, 24/42 Canada–Norway, and 15/28 Slovakia–USA proposals. The remaining 43/91 proposals have either no sustained cap track or only tracks whose median patch appearance score is below the model threshold. This is a source-only review split, **not** a measurement of 48 true throws or 43 false alarms.

The Slovakia [difference contact sheet](slovakia-edit-augmented-development/candidate-difference-contact.jpg) illustrates why the extra checks matter. Three newly proposed red events fall on player close-ups or cutaways (T031, T064, T091); all three have cap-colour tracks but no stone-like patch support. The fourth new unmatched candidate, yellow T083, has stone-like patches on an overhead view and may continue an earlier candidate across a camera transition. Neither appearance nor interval overlap proves a distinct throw. At least some counter-backed tracks also fail the patch model, so this diagnostic cannot safely be used as a universal hard veto. The next filter should combine stone-like appearance with **release-phase passage and cross-view continuity**, while retaining unresolved events for review.

The held-stone-to-motion release scanner was also run on all 91 augmented visual-only windows. The provenance-checked joined evidence tables show **19 with both appearance and a unique colour-compatible release cue, 29 appearance only, six release only, and 37 with neither**. All 91 remain in the review handoff. The scanner has fixed Beijing lower-ice geometry and can miss real off-camera releases. Crucially, the Korea–China red candidate exposed by the S372 split has **neither** cue under these models, despite its source-frame red delivery evidence. A two-cue hard filter would remove a plausible recall gain. The appropriate next discriminator is a physically grounded release-to-receiving-stone link, with uncertainty retained where the broadcast cuts away.

A separate [broadcast-counter consistency audit](slovakia-edit-augmented-development/visual-only-counter-gap-screen.json) helps screen the Slovakia differences without claiming physical event truth. T031 and T091 are red proposals between continuous counter states with **no intervening red decrement**. T064 overlaps the evidence interval for already counted red B52 and may be a duplicate. T083 falls across a state gap that could contain an unobserved yellow decrement. The Korea–China red S372 proposal falls in a counter-state gap/reset and remains unresolved. Across the three recordings, six visual-only proposals have the continuous-state contradiction and three overlap compatible counter evidence. The multiscale handoffs now carry appearance, release and counter-consistency statuses together; none is a hard rejection or a simulation-ready state.
