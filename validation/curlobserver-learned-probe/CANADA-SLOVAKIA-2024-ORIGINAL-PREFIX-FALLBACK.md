# Canada–Slovakia 2024: a path-prefix miss among screen abstentions

The frozen [fourth-recording screen](CANADA-SLOVAKIA-2024-DROPOUT-TRANSFER-RESULTS.md) abstained on candidate S02. Its original receiving-camera scan contained a red candidate path prefix at 1743.0–1745.0 s: five samples, approximately **3.31 m of conditional projected travel**, before two plausible continuations split at 1745.5 s. A broader dropout retry introduced competing paths earlier and returned no shared prefix. That retry did not disprove the narrower original observation; it exposed a conservative evidence-loss failure mode.

The [source-frame sheet](../throw-event-benchmark/canada-slovakia-2024/slovakia-S02-left-prefix-development.jpg) shows a red stone travelling down the left camera's right side at 1743–1746 s. The frozen appearance model, applied separately to three original prefix samples, accepts the first two patches and rejects the third; the [scores and hashes](canada-slovakia-2024-base-prefix-patch-development.json) are preserved. This supports a **moving red stone in the receiving view**, not physical identity with the earlier S02 passage candidate at 1738.55 s. The candidate-to-path link, motion after the ambiguity, and exact release and settle times remain unverified.

The new [prefix-only proposal](canada-slovakia-2024-original-prefix-fallback-development.json) retains S02 because the original ambiguous path has at least five samples, covers at least 70% of the best original path, travels at least 2 m in conditional coordinates, and has at least two positive patches among three scored prefix patches. The [implementation](../../prototype/candidate_prefix_fallback.py) runs only on screen abstentions whose broader retry also abstained. It **does not modify the frozen 17-event result** or claim an eighteenth confirmed throw. This rule was developed after inspecting S02 and has no independent event precision/recall estimate.

The remaining screen abstentions lack comparable selected path prefixes under this audit. A new untouched recording is needed to test whether retaining strong original prefixes improves recall without adding player/equipment false positives. Until then, S02 is a source-supported review priority and a concrete design correction, not a validated production event.
