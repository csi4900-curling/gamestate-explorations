# Non-origin adjacent-motion rescue: first transfers

The end-seven [failure audit](END7-FAILURE-AUDIT.md) found a red-stone delivery in `S465`, a camera shot rejected by the frozen origin-view classifier. We developed a source-only fallback on that exposed case: consider an origin-negative shot with probability at least 0.2, at least 30 seconds after the previous origin-positive shot, and substantial same-colour cap motion in that shot and the immediately following shot. The rule emits at most one colour when only one qualified colour is shared. Same-colour motion is **not** physical stone identity.

The [end-seven development run](norway-end7-nonorigin-continuity-development.json) emits one candidate, red `S465`. The [first end-eight run](norway-end8-nonorigin-continuity-first.json), using unchanged code and inputs, emits none. This fallback therefore does not improve the end-eight counter proxy.

We then applied the frozen rule to two other exposed 2022 recordings using their existing full-source cap-motion and origin-view files, without reading scoreboard colours. The first [Slovakia–USA transfer](slovakia-nonorigin-continuity-first.json) emits **zero** candidates. The first [Sweden–Switzerland transfer](swiss-nonorigin-continuity-first.json) emits **four**: `S171` red, `S322` red, `S404` yellow, and `S566` yellow. Manual inspection of source frames shows `S171` contains a red stone near the delivery player, while `S404` shows the house and then a player cutaway and `S566` mainly shows players moving. These are warning signs that adjacent same-colour tracks alone select non-delivery activity. `S322` shows a red stone in flight before a player cutaway; whether it is a new event or a duplicate of an earlier cue needs event-level review. This inspection is developer analysis, not independent labelling or a precision estimate.

The fallback remains **unpromoted**. It is too sparse to establish recall and admits plausible false positives on another recording. A selected [camera-relative object-motion diagnostic](CAMERA-RELATIVE-OBJECT-MOTION.md) shows that cap appearance plus background registration can reject one house-view pan while preserving moving-stone evidence; two other cases remain unscorable. The next useful successor needs a verified stone object track and a release-to-travel phase transition, with explicit duplicate handling across cuts. The reserved independent Wetzikon source was not used.

A [phase-aware v2 correction](NONORIGIN-PHASE-V2.md) retrospectively removes the three questionable Switzerland candidates while retaining the Norway and Switzerland delivery-view cues. It was added after seeing the first-transfer failures, so it remains development work.
