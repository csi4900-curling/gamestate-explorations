# Competing early-gate transit tie-break

This source-only rule was developed **after** inspecting the exposed end-seven S02 miss. In origin shot `S462`, both colours form five-point early-gate tracks, so the frozen colour rule abstains. Yellow travels 74.24 px with median patch score 0.753; red travels 38.83 px with score 0.173. The new rule chooses a colour only when the stronger transit travels at least 60 px, at least 1.6× farther than its competitor, has patch score at least 0.5 and at least 0.4 higher, and follows a nearly straight path. It otherwise preserves the original decision. This is a cue-quality heuristic, not a release-time detector.

The [end-seven development decisions](norway-end7-gate-tiebreak-events-development.json) change only `S462`, from an abstention to yellow. A retrospective delayed-counter comparison would then pair 11 of 12 clean updates with 11 matching colours; this is **postscore fitting**, not validation. It also does nothing for the next counter-backed miss, S03: that camera shot was excluded by the upstream origin-view classifier, as described in the [failure audit](END7-FAILURE-AUDIT.md).

The rule code was frozen before the [first end-eight source-only scan](norway-end8-dual-gate-first.json) and [first routed decisions](norway-end8-dual-gate-events-first.json) were compared with counters. The [end-eight tie-break output](norway-end8-gate-tiebreak-events-first.json) makes **zero changes** to the 16 coloured candidates. The unchanged mirrored-gate [counter proxy](norway-end8-dual-gate-counter-proxy-first.json) pairs eight of nine clean updates; seven paired colours agree and one conflicts (`S610` paired to S13). Eight visual candidates are unpaired. Seven of the 16 official deliveries have no clean counter anchor. Proxy precision 8/16 and proxy recall 8/9 are not physical throw precision or recall; counter pairings may select the wrong episode.

The first transfer offers no evidence that this tie-break improves a new end. It remains an unpromoted development experiment. Both ends are from the same exposed match, and the reserved independent source was not used.
