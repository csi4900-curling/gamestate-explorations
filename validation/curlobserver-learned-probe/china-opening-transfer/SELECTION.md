# Negative opening-interval development transfer

The first 290 s of Canada–China 2022 were inspected from source-only
five-second sheets before running the dense learned tracker or v3 context
screen. They show broadcast introduction, teams and pre-play setup, with no
visible delivered stone; the final 10 s of the 0–300 s sheet are excluded
because play is about to begin. The frozen reference has zero throw windows.

This interval is a precision stress test for false events in non-play footage,
not a recall test or independent validation. Run the unchanged five-hertz
public-box tracker and revised screen, preserve first raw/screen/score files,
and count every confirmed candidate as a false positive. The v3 screen adds a
minimum of five image steps over four pixels to reject tracks whose apparent
displacement is mostly a camera-cut jump followed by stationary observations.
The condition was chosen after auditing the already-exposed 3600–3900 s gap.
