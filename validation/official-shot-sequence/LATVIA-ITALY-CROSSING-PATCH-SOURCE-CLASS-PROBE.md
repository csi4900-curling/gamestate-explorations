# Frozen crossing-patch model on source-reviewed Latvia and Italy pixels

The [frozen China-trained crossing-patch model](identity-crossing-transfer-v1/model.npz) was applied unchanged to three tracked 48 × 48 crops nearest each selected event crossing (−0.2 s, crossing, +0.2 s). The [selection](latvia-italy-crossing-patch-source-class-selection.json) includes all 13 *accepted* Latvia left candidates with a clear existing source-pixel review (12 stone-shaped, one person) and the exact-source [Italy S03 moving-stone path](../throw-event-benchmark/canada-italy/SHAPE-VETO-S03-SOURCE-RESULT.md). The [packet manifest](latvia-italy-crossing-patch-source-class-packet/manifest.json) records video and timeline fingerprints plus all crop hashes. The [Latvia contact sheet](latvia-italy-crossing-patch-source-class-packet/YCfdKLu-BRc-contact.jpg) and [Italy contact sheet](latvia-italy-crossing-patch-source-class-packet/Vp3TIdOAlrc-contact.jpg) make the tracked pixels inspectable.

The [score artifact](latvia-italy-frozen-crossing-patch-source-class-score.json) gives a positive mean score to **11 of 13** stone-shaped paths and a negative score to the one person path, **Latvia S45**. Italy S03 is positive (**+0.425**) even though the simpler Latvia body-support cutoff rejects it. Latvia **S35** (−0.400) and **S49** (−0.692) are visible stone bodies in the packet but score negative. A zero-score hard veto would therefore discard these two reviewed stone paths. This is an object-appearance diagnostic, **not** event precision, recall or F1: source classes were assigned by the same agent after proposals, the sample contains just one clear person, and a stone-shaped pixel does not certify a delivered stone. The [earlier Estonia/Italy transfer](IDENTITY-PATCH-TRANSFER.md) also found stone-retention failures.

The actionable use is a graded identity cue or review priority, with negative scores kept as abstentions until stronger evidence resolves them. No detector decisions or game-state claims change here. To reproduce, run `python prototype/scripts/select_latvia_italy_crossing_patch_probe.py`, then `feasibility/scripts/python.sh prototype/scripts/build_2024_crossing_patch_packet.py --selection validation/official-shot-sequence/latvia-italy-crossing-patch-source-class-selection.json --output <new-packet-dir>`, and `feasibility/scripts/python.sh prototype/scripts/score_latvia_italy_frozen_crossing_patches.py --packet <new-packet-dir> --output <new-score.json>`. The packet builder refuses to overwrite its output; the score runner and saved hashes fix the exact frozen model and feature code used in this result. A separate score rerun from the saved packet produced byte-identical JSON.
