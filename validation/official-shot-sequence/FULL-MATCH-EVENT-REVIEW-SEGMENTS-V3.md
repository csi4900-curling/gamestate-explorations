The [v3 review export](pwg2022-full-match-event-review-segments-v3-preorigin-travel-development.json) builds one context window from each candidate's named source camera shots. The [browser viewer](../../prototype/full-match-event-review-v3/index.html) searches all 359 windows; enter `TRAVEL-B02` to show the new case. The B02 review window is 354.0–387.2 s, with observed receiving travel at 365.4–380.2 s. These are review bounds and image-space track times, not measured physical throw start, release, or settle times.

The [full unassigned-shot audit](PWG2022-ALL-UNASSIGNED-SHOT-SCAN.md) records the frozen 1,351-shot selection and explains why the source cue in S28 is receiving travel rather than a held-origin release. The B02 selection rule was developed after source review on exposed recordings. It has not established independent physical throw precision, recall, F1, cross-view identity, or a simulation-ready trajectory.
