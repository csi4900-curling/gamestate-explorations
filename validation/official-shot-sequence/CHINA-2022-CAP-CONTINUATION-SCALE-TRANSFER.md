# 2022 camera-family cap-continuation scale audit

The [one-step audit](china-2022-cap-continuation-scale-transfer-development.json) tested whether the Czech 2024 local-cap continuation rule transfers to the different 2022 Canada–China broadcast camera family after **geometry-only resolution scaling** from 640 × 360 to 1920 × 1080 coordinates. It reconstructed all **160** frozen shot-scoped cap tracks exactly from source frames and refused to extend 17 tracks whose next half-second sample would cross the camera-shot boundary. The remaining **143** in-shot tracks were each probed one frame forward with the unchanged Czech HSV colours, constant-velocity gate and equivalent pixel-size thresholds. This is a continuation diagnostic on cap tracks, not event detection or an independently labelled stone-tracking score.

The naïvely scaled absolute cap-area gate yielded only **8** unique next-cap choices, **1** competing-cap abstention and **134** no-cap abstentions. In 116 of those 134, the source frame contained one or more raw same-colour components in the search crop, but all were larger than the scaled Czech contour-area maximum of **700/9 ≈ 77.8 source pixels²**. The raw component-area median was **168 source pixels²**. Thus the cap's *apparent* size changes with broadcast camera scale and framing; multiplying 2024 pixel thresholds by the resolution ratio alone does not preserve object size. The [S28 source frames](china-2022-cap-scale-source-probe/s28-r01-source.jpg) show a red stone with a 222-pixel² next-frame cap that the area gate excludes. Its center also exceeds the fixed relative-motion growth limit by a small margin, so relaxing size alone would still abstain there.

The [post-inspection area sensitivity](china-2022-cap-area-gate-sensitivity-development.json) compares the same 143 recorded next-frame components with causal gates based on the *previous cap's observed area*. Allowing the next contour to be 0.5–2× the previous area produces **109** unique motion-gate choices and **34** no-cap abstentions; a 0.75–1.5× interval gives **106** unique choices. Without any area limit there are 111 unique choices and two competing-cap abstentions. These are **candidate step counts, not true-positive counts**. The previous-size rule was proposed after this failure and has not been validated on an unseen 2022 recording.

Two source cases show why a relative area check is more useful than simply removing the limit. [S86](china-2022-cap-scale-source-probe/s86-r01-source.jpg) contains a 172-pixel² red stone cap and a nearby 853-pixel² patch of red house paint in the next search crop; the fixed 2024 size maximum removes both, whereas a prior-size check retains the cap and excludes the paint. [S230](china-2022-cap-scale-source-probe/s230-y01-source.jpg) shows a yellow stone passing over coloured ice artwork. Its next yellow mask merges with the artwork and jumps from 46.5 to 165 pixel², so the relative-size check abstains rather than trusting a shifted colour centroid. These are selected, same-agent visual diagnoses, not independent correctness labels.

The current 2022 shot-scoped event pipeline already respects camera cuts; any future cap-continuation adapter must retain that boundary, model apparent cap scale from past observations, and report occlusion or artwork merging as uncertainty. It must then be tested on different view types and an independently labelled recording before contributing to physical throw F1 or game-state identity. Neither this one-step audit nor the area sensitivity supplies a complete trajectory, settled board, release time, spin count or simulation-ready event.

A separate [camera-relative motion transfer](CHINA-2022-ICE-REGION-REGISTRATION-TRANSFER.md) now tests short-frame, ice-like-region registration on all frozen China cap paths. It improves source-only motion-cue coverage but does not resolve physical throw identity or F1.
