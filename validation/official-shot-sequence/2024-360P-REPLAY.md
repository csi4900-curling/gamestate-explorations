# 2024 source-variant replay of reviewed events

The published [Canada–China video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y_U6gzqORUs)
was downloaded as YouTube format 134 (640×360, 30 fps, 8177 s). It is cached
at `validation/source-cache/Y_U6gzqORUs-360p.mp4` (ignored by Git). Its SHA-256
is `51d300ede336efdd8d39029f8728c9d7cac3dd0fcfdc96eb7b84832d80753e82`,
which **differs** from the saved whole-match timeline's source hash. These
are separate source variants; the old timeline was not edited or relabelled.

I replayed the unchanged span candidate, local-motion and confirmation stages
on three windows selected after the [source review](../throw-event-benchmark/layout-router/full-2024-coverage/FINE-EVENT-REVIEW.md).
The [source frames](china-360p-reviewed-frames.jpg) at ±0.5 s show the
tracked object at each crossing: a travelling red stone, a red broom head,
and a red mark on a player, respectively.

| [920–955](china-360p-confirmed-920-955.json) | 935.777 s red | [1](china-360p-raw-920-955.json) | 935.777 s red | Real delivery. |
| [5865–5898](china-360p-confirmed-5865-5898.json) | 5881.729 s red | [2](china-360p-raw-5865-5898.json) | 5881.729 s red | Likely broom-led false event. |

The exact three timestamps match the saved event times despite the byte-level
source difference. The [motion measurements](china-360p-motion-920-955.json)
and analogous `motion-5865-5898.json` / `motion-6478-6520.json` artifacts
retain the unchanged confirmation inputs. This replay shows that the known
semantic false positives survive a resolution/encoding variant. It is a
prediction-targeted development regression, not an independent precision or
recall measurement; it does not characterize the rest of the recording.

The published [Canada–Estonia video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PXGfMeiTKMA)
was separately downloaded in the same format. Its cached source hash is
`fec4333caceca51468ad5387c0fafafcaf13ee59c51ed78425976c22b3bfff3b`,
also different from its saved timeline hash. The unchanged detector reproduced
both reviewed times in [6250–6300 raw candidates](estonia-360p-raw-6250-6300.json)
and [confirmed both](estonia-360p-confirmed-6250-6300.json): the real red
delivery at 6261.075 s and the likely broom-led false event at 6280.083 s.
The [motion measurements](estonia-360p-motion-6250-6300.json) and
[source frames](estonia-360p-reviewed-frames.jpg) preserve that replay.
This second same-production match confirms the failure is not unique to the
China recording; it remains a selected development check.

The existing cap proposal's neutral-body support cannot rescue these cases:
the saved China real stone has mean support 0.761, while its broom and player
false events have 0.887 and 0.892. Estonia's real stone has 0.986 and its
broom false event 0.965. A higher support threshold would lose the China
real throw before rejecting either China false event.

The next detector needs evidence that a newly delivered *stone* moves away
from the delivery area. Broom tips and marks on moving people cannot be
rejected reliably from colour, velocity, or receiving-view cap motion alone.
