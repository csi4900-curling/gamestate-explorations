# First cross-recording adjacent-board transfer

After freezing the Swiss end-two adjacent-board scan rule, we predeclared the same shot-number window, **shots 10–14 of end two**, in the Canada–China 2022 recording. All five shots had clean counter anchors. The [source-only first scan](china-end2-adjacent-video-boards-first.json) used the same frame offsets, next-counter buffer, overhead detector, stone detector, temporal stability requirement, and previously source-inferred top orientation. It was saved before official board positions were loaded.

The scan accepted one overhead frame for shot 10 but produced no stable stones; shots 11 and 12 produced one stable stone each; shots 13 and 14 had no accepted overhead frames. In the separate [first diagram comparison](china-end2-adjacent-board-comparison-first.json), each emitted stone matches an official symbol, but both corresponding official boards have **three stones**. The outcome is **two partial boards from five shots, zero complete boards**. This is a selected same-production-family transfer, not a general board recall estimate.

The [source-only local comparison](china-end2-partial-board-transitions-first.json) sees one red observation after shot 11 and one yellow after shot 12, with no same-colour position match. Because both views omit two official stones, the appearance/disappearance is a **change in partial visibility**, not a proven physical takeout or addition. The counter-linked event candidate for shot 12 also has conflicting colour evidence, so a colour-based causal interpretation would be especially unsafe.

The first comparison JSON inherited one inaccurate limitation sentence from the Swiss scoring script: it says this China window was selected around a prior successful board. That describes the Swiss development window, **not this transfer**. The China window was chosen from counter-anchor availability before inspecting these frames or diagram positions. The first-run JSON is preserved; this note corrects its selection provenance.

Together with the [Swiss adjacent-board study](ADJACENT-BOARD-TRANSITIONS.md), this shows a concrete event-to-board sequence on favorable views and a coverage failure on another recording. The next board-state detector must explicitly measure visible ice coverage and combine multiple views before claiming complete state. Neither result supplies persistent physical stone IDs, exact settle times, or simulation-ready states. The reserved independent source was not used.

A later [wide-view image-space diagnostic](WIDE-VIEW-PERSPECTIVE-EVIDENCE.md) finds two red and one yellow stable cap-like objects in four frames after China shot 12, matching the official diagram's colour counts. Its elliptical view remains uncalibrated, so those observations are not silently merged into the board export.
