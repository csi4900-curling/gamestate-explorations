# Full source scan of camera shots outside the v2 event union

The [selection plan](pwg2022-all-unassigned-shots-source-scan-plan.json) froze **every camera shot not named by any event** in the additive 358-candidate union across the three exposed 2022 recordings. It did not filter by the origin model, counters, cue colour, or source review. The unchanged held-to-motion rule and ice-context check scanned all **1,351** selected shots. The [reconciliation output](pwg2022-all-unassigned-shots-source-scan-summary-development.json) verifies source hashes and shot selection and reproduces all **69** origin-model-positive shots still outside the v2 union from the earlier frozen scan exactly.

The four supported cues require different interpretations. The [Swiss source frames](swiss-model-negative-cue-review.jpg) show S97 as a delivery-side red-stone view immediately before the existing red `VISUAL-S98` candidate; [adjacent frames](swiss-adjacent-candidate-review.jpg) show S98 as a close-up of the same red-stone phase, so S97 is a plausible *precursor*, not a new throw count. S221 is an overhead house view; its cap passage is not a held-origin release even though a red post-cut motion cue follows. S332 is a long team-side view with stone handling immediately before existing red `VISUAL-S333`; its physical relation remains ambiguous. All three have an origin-model-positive **following** shot, not a preceding one.

China S28 is an overhead receiving view. The rule's red “held-to-motion” trigger at 379.2 s is **not a release cue** in that view. [Source frames](china-s28-receiving-travel-review.jpg) show a red stone moving down the ice. Immediately preceding S27 is an unassigned origin-model-positive delivery view; [its source frames](china-s27-origin-context-review.jpg) show the red stone at the thrower. The existing receiving cap track `S28-R01` spans **365.4–380.2 s**, **223.46 pixels** over 65 observations. The raw broadcast counter's unassigned red B02 update is at **383.0 s**, just after S28 ends at 382.2 s. This is a strong provisional missing-event lead, but not proof of physical stone identity across the cut or an exact release time.

The [structural triage](pwg2022-preorigin-travel-rescue-proposals-development.json) applies the same postscan rule to all four supported shots: require an unassigned origin-predicted predecessor, verified adjacent cut, one substantial same-colour receiving cap track, and one same-colour unassigned delayed counter update. It proposes only China `TRAVEL-B02`. This rule was developed after viewing exposed cases, so its one proposal is **development evidence**, not a frozen transfer result or independently measured event F1. The [v2 union](pwg2022-provisional-event-evidence-union-v2-source-rescues-development.json) remains frozen at 358 candidates; a separate [additive v3 union](pwg2022-provisional-event-evidence-union-v3-preorigin-travel-development.json) carries B02 as its 359th provisional candidate.

The scan does not establish recall. A camera shot can contain an obscured or off-corridor throw without satisfying the cap passage rule. Nor is this a false-positive estimate: the “unassigned” set may include views of existing throws. The B02 proposal has no verified physical release, persistent stone ID, settled board, or simulation-ready trajectory.
