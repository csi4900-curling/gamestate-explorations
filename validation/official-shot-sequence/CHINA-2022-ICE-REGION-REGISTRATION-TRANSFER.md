# China 2022 transfer of the Norway camera-motion cue

The [full-source transfer](china-ice-region-cap-track-registration-transfer-development.json) applied three unchanged camera-relative motion variants to **all 160** frozen China 2022 shot-scoped cap paths in a second 640 × 360 broadcast recording. It exactly reconstructed each candidate path from source frames and used the same approximately one-second point-pair selector, 0.25-second chaining, all-subpair registration rule, five-source-pixel residual threshold and two-consecutive-pair cue defined during the Norway 2022 audit. The ice-like region parameters were also unchanged: largest bright low-saturation component, 41-pixel morphological close, at least 20% frame coverage and 30-pixel target exclusion. China had been exposed in prior project development, though these registration settings were fixed before this transfer; the result is **not** an independent event benchmark.

| Source-only diagnostic on 998 point pairs / 160 paths | Direct 1 s registration | 0.25 s chained registration | 0.25 s plus ice-like region |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Pairs with usable registration | 247 | 502 | 517 |
| Paths with sustained relative-motion cue | 4 | 55 | 65 |
| Paths with weak-motion cue | 37 | 23 | 16 |
| Paths abstaining from insufficient or mixed registration | 119 | 82 | 79 |

These are **registration coverage and candidate-path cues**, not stone true positives, throws, or precision/recall. A real stone can be missed, a path can change physical identity, and an existing stone can move after a collision. Camera cuts remain hard boundaries in the frozen catalog.

The [fixed transition packet](china-registration-transfer-source-probe/manifest.json) includes the **only** path losing a sustained cue after restricting registration to the ice-like region, the four largest-span new ice-region cues, and the four largest-span new short-pair cues. On their terminal frames, [S544](china-registration-transfer-source-probe/s544-r01-source.jpg), [S191](china-registration-transfer-source-probe/s191-r01-source.jpg), [S409](china-registration-transfer-source-probe/s409-r01-source.jpg) and [S198](china-registration-transfer-source-probe/s198-r01-source.jpg) visibly track stones. Terminal frames alone cannot establish motion, because the tracked stone may already be settled.

The follow-up [motion-interval packet](china-registration-motion-interval-source-probe/manifest.json) therefore samples frames **inside the consecutive high-residual runs** for the four largest-span new short-pair cues. [S307](china-registration-motion-interval-source-probe/s307-r01-motion.jpg), [S230](china-registration-motion-interval-source-probe/s230-y01-motion.jpg), [S45](china-registration-motion-interval-source-probe/s45-r01-motion.jpg) and [S86](china-registration-motion-interval-source-probe/s86-r01-motion.jpg) visibly follow moving stones through source rink markings in those selected intervals. The sole ice-region downgrade, [S412](china-registration-motion-interval-source-probe/s412-r01-motion.jpg), follows a stone that stays fixed relative to the house as the camera framing changes; its whole-frame short-pair cue includes two >5-pixel residuals, while the ice-region residuals over that interval are below one pixel. This source check supports the downgrade on that case. Both packets were selected after inspecting transfer statuses and are same-agent development review, not independent labels of all paths.

The China result reproduces the Norway direction of change: short frame intervals materially improve registration coverage, and restricting features to an ice-like region adds more usable pairs while suppressing at least one camera-motion false cue. It does **not** prove a robust throw detector. The image mask is not a calibrated rink plane; foreground objects can contaminate it, and some camera views yield no registration. Before this cue controls segmentation, the project needs independent physical throw labels and identity checks across complete paths, including non-overhead views. The cue should remain additive evidence with explicit abstention.

Reproduce from the project root with `feasibility/scripts/python.sh prototype/scripts/transfer_china_ice_region_camera_registration.py`, `feasibility/scripts/python.sh prototype/scripts/render_china_registration_transfer_cases.py`, and `feasibility/scripts/python.sh prototype/scripts/render_china_registration_motion_intervals.py`, moving existing outputs aside first. The artifacts pin source, catalog, Norway rule and runner hashes.
