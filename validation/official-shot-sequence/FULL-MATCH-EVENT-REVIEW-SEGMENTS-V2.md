# Full-match review windows with source-only release rescues

The [additive v2 event union](pwg2022-provisional-event-evidence-union-v2-source-rescues-development.json) contains **358 provisional candidates** across the three exposed 2022 recordings: 118 Switzerland, 124 China and 116 Slovakia. It preserves the [frozen 356-candidate baseline](pwg2022-provisional-event-evidence-union-development.json) and adds two separate source-only candidates: Swiss `SOURCE-S392` and China `SOURCE-S216`. Each has a held-to-motion cap cue over ice, a verified broadcast cut, and a unique same-colour strict receiving-view track. Neither has a counter or official shot assignment. Physical stone identity across the cut remains unverified.

The [v2 review export](pwg2022-full-match-event-review-segments-v2-source-rescues-development.json) derives one review window from each candidate's named camera shots. The [browser viewer](../../prototype/full-match-event-review-v2/index.html) searches all 358 windows; enter `SOURCE-S392` or `SOURCE-S216` to show the new cases. Those windows are source-video context, not measured physical throw start and end times. Exact release, settled board and simulation-ready trajectory remain unknown.

The [outside-union source audit](PWG2022-OUTSIDE-UNION-ORIGIN-CUES.md) records the frozen 71-shot selection, the two source-only cues, the rejected close-up, and the receiving-view evidence. This is development candidate coverage, not independently labeled full-match precision, recall or F1.

Rebuild with `python prototype/scripts/export_2022_source_release_rescue_union_v2.py`, then `python prototype/scripts/export_2022_full_match_review_segments_v2.py`, then `python prototype/scripts/build_full_match_event_review.py --input validation/official-shot-sequence/pwg2022-full-match-event-review-segments-v2-source-rescues-development.json --output prototype/full-match-event-review-v2/index.html --method-link FULL-MATCH-EVENT-REVIEW-SEGMENTS-V2.md` from the project root.
