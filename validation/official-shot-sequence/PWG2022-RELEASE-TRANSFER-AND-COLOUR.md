# Full-match candidate release-cue transfer and two-view colour handoff

The [frozen transfer plan](pwg2022-provisional-release-transfer-plan.json) pins the unchanged held-to-motion cue, source hashes, and **all** provisional candidate windows before the Swiss and China first runs. The [Swiss](swiss-provisional-release-transfer-first.json) and [China](china-provisional-release-transfer-first.json) outputs use that rule without retuning. The earlier [Slovakia development output](slovakia-provisional-release-coverage-development.json) uses the same cue. These are exposed recordings; this is cue coverage on a candidate ledger, not independent physical throw F1.

The cue appears once in **111/356 (31.2%)** provisional windows. That is sparse but source-linked timing evidence for selected events. It is **not throw recall**: the ledger can contain false candidates and miss physical throws. The 101 colour agreements are internal consistency with a provisional ledger, not independent precision. Two conflicts show why a cap passage cannot be promoted automatically: in Slovakia S146, [source frames](slovakia-s146-release-conflict.jpg) show multiple stones around the house; in China S593, [source frames](china-s593-source-colour-conflict.jpg) show a yellow-handled stone in the delivery view although the ledger says red.

The [two-view source-colour handoff](pwg2022-cross-view-source-colour-handoff-development.json) joins a unique within-view passage to the previously frozen strict post-cut colour cue by the same source shot. Among 111 passage-bearing windows, the post-cut sidecar supplies matching source colour in **16** cases and supplies no contradictory strict colour in that overlap. The sixteen include **four** ledger-unknown events (China S529; Slovakia S192, S340 and S411) and **one** ledger conflict (China S593). For S593, both independent image-view cues say yellow, while the old visual-motion ledger says red. This supports a **provisional yellow colour correction for review**, preserved in the sidecar without overwriting the ledger. It does not yet prove that the same physical stone persists across the cut.

A [temporal handoff audit](pwg2022-two-view-temporal-handoff-development.json) reruns the unchanged receiving-view scanner on all sixteen source-colour agreements and verifies each strict track count against the frozen post-cut sidecar. Every case has exactly one qualifying same-colour track. First observed motion starts **0.2–4.8 s after the cut**; China S593 starts after 0.6 s. These gaps make the two observations temporally compatible. However, **all sixteen track registrations are unresolved by the current background-motion check**. A matching colour and near-immediate track cannot establish that it is the same physical stone; S593 remains a provisional correction and none of these are continuous trajectories.

The selected [S593 background-registration audit](china-s593-background-registration-development.json) explains one abstention. For frames at 7954.8 and 7955.8 s, the unmasked corner tracker has 17 tracked features and 11 affine inliers. After excluding the yellow stone and a 30-pixel margin, only **seven** features remain tracked—too few even to fit the same model. Most apparent inliers were on or near the target, so lowering the existing 30-inlier reliability threshold would risk treating stone motion as camera motion. The receiving shot shows largely plain ice with a long centre line; that geometry gives limited longitudinal motion information. This is a postscore diagnostic on one case, not a calibrated trajectory.

These outputs give a downstream state reconstructor explicit source-colour evidence, cue time, counter provenance, disagreement status, and false flags for verified physical identity and simulation readiness. No counter link, event count, exact release timestamp, board transition or simulation-ready state is changed. Reproduce the colour handoff with `python prototype/scripts/export_pwg2022_cross_view_source_colours.py`; the frozen first transfer is reproduced by running `PYTHONPATH=prototype feasibility/scripts/python.sh -u prototype/scripts/audit_pwg2022_provisional_release_transfer.py PREL000101` and then `PREL000102` from the project root.
