# Right-view proposals across the supported Canada–Estonia 2024 match

The [predeclared scan plan](RIGHT-LARGE-CAP-FULL-MATCH-PLAN.md) fixed the 1 Hz larger-cap/global-assignment rule and routed coverage before the full-run scans. The existing [left-view match timeline](../layout-router/full-2024-coverage/new-preflight-estonia24/timeline.json) has **128 provisional events** over two supported split-view runs, `[90,3870)` and `[3960,8370)` seconds. The other **243 seconds** remain explicit camera/layout gaps. This is a development pass on the same exposed recording, not independent throw evaluation.

The right-view scans preserve [68 R01 proposals](right-large-cap-full-r01-tail.json) and [84 R02 proposals](right-large-cap-full-r02-tail.json), **152** in total. The moving-tail derivative shortens 29 R01 and 26 R02 long stationary-plus-moving prefixes without creating new source observations. The [full supported-run temporal inventory](right-large-cap-full-supported-temporal-inventory.json) uses the declared 20-second direction-aware time/colour window and keeps all proposals. It finds **124** right paths with one provisional left candidate, **28** right paths with none, and **four** left events with no right candidate. None of these counts is an event precision, recall, or F1 measurement.

All 28 right-only proposals were rendered from source frames in [R01 sheets](right-large-cap-full-r01-disagreement-source-review/manifest.json) and [R02 sheets](right-large-cap-full-r02-disagreement-source-review/manifest.json), then [same-agent source-pixel classes](right-large-cap-full-r01-disagreement-source-review/source-prefix-classes.json) and [R02 classes](right-large-cap-full-r02-disagreement-source-review/source-prefix-classes.json) were saved **before** context scoring:

| Right path without a left time/colour candidate | R01 | R02 | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Moving stone pixels | 4 | 0 | 4 |
| Person, wheelchair, or equipment pixels | 4 | 19 | 23 |
| Stone moved by a person during late handling | 0 | 1 | 1 |

The four moving-stone paths occur near 120, 249, 576 and 3866 s. The [left-context sheet](right-large-cap-full-right-only-stone-left-context/manifest.json) shows nearby ice/play views for all four. They are **candidate left-detector misses or timing/coverage disagreements**, not four verified missed throws. The 3866 s path is within four seconds of the supported-run boundary, so an absent later left event is particularly ambiguous. The late manually handled stone near 8240 s illustrates why motion plus clear ice context is insufficient to call a delivery.

The four left-only candidates were shown in a separate [source sheet](right-large-cap-full-left-only-source-review/manifest.json) and [reviewed](right-large-cap-full-left-only-source-review/source-prefix-classes.json). Their marked pixels appear to follow one person/wheelchair and three brooms or other equipment. They are developer-reviewed likely false left proposals, not independently certified negatives.

The fixed paired context cue was run on the 28 source-reviewed right-only paths: [R01](right-large-cap-full-r01-disagreement-combined-context.json) keeps all four moving-stone paths clear and flags its four person/equipment paths; [R02](right-large-cap-full-r02-disagreement-combined-context.json) flags all 19 person/equipment paths **but leaves the person-handled stone clear**. Thus the cue helps triage people here, but cannot separate a throw from other stone handling.

The [unreviewed context sweep](right-large-cap-full-supported-unreviewed-context.json) covers all 152 paths. Of the 124 time-matched paths, 122 are clear and two are cluttered. Both cluttered paths visibly track people in the [targeted source sheet](right-large-cap-full-r02-matched-cluttered-source-review/manifest.json); their temporal agreement with left events was misleading. Before inspecting clear paths, a deterministic time-spread [12-of-122 selection](right-large-cap-full-matched-clear-source-sample/selection.json) was saved. All twelve selected paths show moving stone pixels in the [source sheets](right-large-cap-full-matched-clear-source-sample/source-prefix-classes.json). This sample is a development counterexample search, **not** a precision estimate for the other 110 clear paths.

The current evidence supports a high-recall **candidate** pipeline with source and context review tiers. It does not prove complete physical throw segmentation: the full match lacks independent exhaustive labels, cross-camera object identity, validated release/settle boundaries, and post-throw board states. No event was promoted into a simulation-ready game state, and the reserved unseen 2025 end remains untouched.

The [ordered candidate evidence sequence](right-large-cap-full-candidate-evidence-sequence-v2.json) keeps all 128 left proposals, 28 right-only proposals, 152 right paths, context tiers, source-reviewed disagreements, and the 243-second coverage gaps in one machine-readable handoff. Its **156 rows are candidates**, including nonstone paths; none is a confirmed physical throw or complete post-throw game state.

The same fixed right-panel cap sampler was run after all 128 provisional left events at passage +15, +20, and +25 s, with next-event and routed-run guards. The [source-frame scan](right-large-cap-full-post-left-partial-caps.json) has 74 rows with stable coloured cap proposals (112 proposals total), 31 with none, 20 scene/player-motion abstentions, and three next-event abstentions. The [joined partial-state handoff](right-large-cap-full-candidate-partial-state-sequence.json) retains these source-visible partial cap observations beside candidate evidence. It does not assert that a cap is a stone, that the throw has settled, or that the visible right-panel region contains the complete board.

The unchanged [pre-candidate sampler](right-large-cap-full-pre-left-partial-caps.json) finds 78 rows with stable caps, 45 with none, two scene/player-motion abstentions and three previous-event abstentions. Its [pre/post image comparison](right-large-cap-full-pre-post-partial-cap-deltas.json) keeps 99 comparable windows, with 27 window and two cross-window scene abstentions. It records 64 locally persistent cap positions, 46 newly visible caps and 48 no longer visible caps; 66 rows have a nonzero visibility change. The [per-candidate pre/post handoff](right-large-cap-full-candidate-pre-post-image-state-handoff.json) exposes these as image evidence only. No count here verifies stone identity, a complete settled board, or the scoring state after a physical delivery.

The transferred fixed ice-context [cap scores](right-large-cap-full-pre-post-cap-context.json) put 14 image-change rows in a clear review tier and 52 in a cluttered tier ([triage](right-large-cap-full-pre-post-cap-delta-context-triage.json)). The [expanded-upper-ROI transfer](../canada-italy/EXPANDED-BOARD-CAP-ROI-RESULTS.md) admits additional house-area caps but increases the cluttered image-change tier to 61. This remains development evidence, not validated post-throw board accuracy.
