# Canada–Scotland 2024: frozen transfer and camera-registration diagnosis

This is an **unscored transfer audit**, not an event F1 result. The 1650–2760 s interval, detector settings, model hashes, and source-only inspection were fixed in the [pre-inference plan](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/CANADA-SCOTLAND-2024-PREFIX-TRANSFER-PLAN.md). No exhaustive source-only reference of physical throws was frozen, and the reserved Canada–USA match was not used. The analysis below separates the unchanged first pass from changes made after seeing its failures.

## Unchanged first pass

The 360p, 10 Hz line-independent span detector generated **32** raw candidates. The fixed x = 160 ± 60 px corridor retained **26**. On the aligned 1080p source, the original candidate-centric motion scan found three unique receiving-view paths. The frozen still-camera hold raised this to 13; bounded-gap selection raised it to 18. Conditional dropout rescue added none. The frozen three-sample stone-patch screen produced **17 provisional motion-and-appearance events**, eight abstentions without a supported path, and one path without stone-like patch support. These are *proposals*, not verified physical deliveries.

The newly frozen [original-prefix fallback](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-scotland-2024-original-prefix-fallback-frozen-first.json) produced **zero** proposals. Its required `ambiguous_far_entry_motion_paths` state was absent in this recording. The Slovakia S02 rule therefore did not address the dominant Scotland failure mode.

Postscore source review shows a yellow stone travelling during candidate S12 and a red stone travelling during S19. It does not establish delivery identity or an exhaustive event count. These examples motivated a camera-registration diagnosis; they cannot be used as independent test labels. The frozen [screen](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-scotland-2024-dropout-screen-frozen-first.json), [motion scan](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-scotland-2024-candidate-centric-motion-frozen-first-sharded.json), [hold](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-scotland-2024-registration-hold-frozen-first-sharded.json), and [bounded-gap selection](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-scotland-2024-motion-with-gaps-frozen-first.json) preserve the original result.

## Postscore registration development

The right camera has a different zoom from the original calibration reference. An anchor frame at 2044.5 s was chosen **after** inspecting the first-pass failures. Transporting the old calibration through this anchor changes the image used for feature registration; it does not independently validate rink geometry. In a fixed diagnostic sample of the first five receiving frames from each of 21 right-view candidates, the [registration audit](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-scotland-2024-rebased-registration-support-development.json) registered **51/105** frames against the old reference and **105/105** against the local one. This is a candidate-local, postscore image-matching result, not video-wide event recall or metric position accuracy.

The [rebased motion scan](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-scotland-2024-candidate-centric-motion-rebased-v3-development.json) used the local frame for registration and warped the original clean reference for coloured-cap detection. It found 16 unique paths among the 26 corridor candidates. The [matching patch check](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-scotland-2024-rebased-v3-patch-registered-development.json) used the *same local registration* and clean background; its [screen](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-scotland-2024-rebased-v3-screen-registered-development.json) provisionally accepted **15** candidates. It adds S19 to the frozen accepted set, but omits S02, S09, and S29. The frozen branch's hold/gap recovery has not been rerun on the rebased branch, so 15 versus 17 is not a like-for-like pipeline comparison. A preliminary rebased patch artifact that used the old registration is superseded by this matching check.

## Fixed-grid camera reference and complete development branch

To remove manual anchor choice from the inference procedure, a deterministic [fixed-grid selector](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-scotland-2024-fixed-grid-right-anchor-development.json) sampled the full source interval every 10 s at a 5 s phase. It clustered accepted right-view registrations by the positions of four projected image corners, then chose the strongest frame from the largest pose cluster. The rule chose **1855.0 s** from a cluster of 28 frames. It uses no throw candidates or event labels, but was **designed after this recording's failures were seen** and is therefore development rather than untouched transfer validation.

On the same candidate-local diagnostic set, the [fixed-grid anchor](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-scotland-2024-fixed-grid-registration-support-development.json) registered **103/105** frames, versus 51/105 with the original reference. The complete matching branch then found **18** direct unique paths; [same-shot hold](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-scotland-2024-fixed-grid-hold-merged-development.json) raised this to **19**, and bounded-gap selection left it at 19. The [gap-path patch check](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-scotland-2024-fixed-grid-gap-patch-development.json) uses the selected anchor for registration, the original clean reference for background subtraction, and explicitly borrowed transforms where the hold audit supports them. [Duplicate checking](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-scotland-2024-fixed-grid-gap-dedup-development.json) found no shared timed paths. The resulting [screen](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-scotland-2024-fixed-grid-gap-screen-development.json) has **18 provisional motion-and-appearance candidates**, seven no-path abstentions, and one path without stone-like appearance.

Candidate by candidate, the fixed-grid branch retains all 17 candidates from the unchanged first pass and adds **S19**. S02 is recovered by the same-shot hold and has a positive sampled stone-patch score. S12 has a selected motion path, but all three sampled patch scores are below the frozen acceptance threshold; its source-visible travelling stone remains a concrete appearance-filter failure to investigate. The 18 candidates are still proposals: there is no independent physical-throw denominator, and S19 could not be promoted to a confirmed delivery from motion and patch evidence alone.

The [artifact-chain verifier](fixed-grid-transfer-verification-development.json) checks source fingerprints, stage links, candidate order, model fingerprint, and the 32 → 26 → 18 accounting. It does not verify physical throws.

## Interpretation and next gate

Registration transfer is a real bottleneck, but more registered frames alone do not prove a better throw detector. A complete, internally consistent fixed-grid branch now exists for this recording; its camera-reference rule and all downstream settings must be frozen and tested on a recording not used to design them. Independent source-only physical-throw labels are still needed for precision, recall, or F1. The current Scotland artifacts support a concrete camera-adaptation design and expose a likely missed travelling-stone candidate plus an appearance-filter failure; they do not validate full-match segmentation, cross-camera identity, settled game states, or simulation-ready paths.
