# Camera-aware right-entry fusion, development replay

The [fusion module](../../../../prototype/camera_aware_right_fusion.py) and [exporter](../../../../prototype/scripts/export_camera_aware_right_fusion.py) combine a right-entry review tier with existing left-event evidence **only where the left camera has source-supported coverage at both event times**. A same-colour left event 0–20 seconds before the right entry can provide a conditional time/colour corroboration. Zero or multiple left matches, multiple right entries claiming the same left event, and unsupported left-camera coverage remain explicit review statuses. No status verifies physical stone identity or accepts a new throw.

| Recording and evidence | Right review entries | Fusion result |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Full Canada–Czechia 2024, 0–6683 s | 45 | 40 conditional cross-view corroborations; 5 unmatched with supported left view |
| Canada–Latvia 2024, static-left 5015–6670 s | 8 | 8 right-only reviews because the left panel is static while the right panel moves |
| Italy 2024, 1650–2760 s | 10 | 10 conditional cross-view corroborations |
| Estonia 2024, 1650–2760 s | 9 | 9 conditional cross-view corroborations |
| China 2024, 1650–2760 s | 10 | 10 conditional cross-view corroborations |
| Scotland 2024, 1650–2760 s, original route | 11 | 7 conditional corroborations; 4 right-only reviews in layout gaps |

The [Czech output](right-entry-transfer/czech-full-camera-aware-fusion.json) uses the full-match right-entry variant, the existing left accepted-event screen, the 90-second layout audit, and the existing partial-state sequence. Its 40 corroborated paths are the 40 short moving-stone paths in the separate same-agent source review; the five unmatched paths are the five visible people/wheelchairs. **35** corroborated paths point to partial camera-local post-event boards. This separation is an observed development result, not an independently measured classifier precision or physical identity proof. The layout audit cannot detect every short camera cut or occlusion.

The [Latvia output](latvia24-full-v1/right-entry-camera-aware-fusion.json) uses the already preserved static-left coverage overlay. All eight right prefixes fall in the 1655-second span marked `left_panel_static_while_right_panel_active`, where the original left timeline has zero observed events. They remain **right-only review candidates** despite the prior source strips showing short moving-stone paths. Requiring a left match for every right path would erase this potential recall route.
Their later right-camera frames now have a separate [partial colour-cap observation audit](LATVIA-RIGHT-ONLY-PARTIAL-CAPS.md): six repeated image-cap sets and two explicit view-motion abstentions. No physical board state is inferred.

Each export preserves input/code hashes, each right path, coverage reason, matching candidate IDs where applicable, board-observation status where available, and `simulation_ready: false`. They do not alter the original left timelines or supply an independently labelled full-match reference. The camera-aware statuses keep corroborated evidence, right-only evidence during static-left or unknown-layout coverage, and unmatched evidence despite left support distinct. [Tests](../../../../prototype/tests/test_camera_aware_right_fusion.py) cover the support switch, duplicate-right ambiguity, and source-hash rejection.

The additional fixed-interval exports for [Italy](right-entry-transfer/italy-1650-2760-camera-aware-fusion.json), [Estonia](right-entry-transfer/estonia-1650-2760-camera-aware-fusion.json), and [China](right-entry-transfer/china-1650-2760-camera-aware-fusion.json) link every right prefix to a distinct same-colour prior left event in supported runs. The [original Scotland route](right-entry-transfer/scotland-1650-2760-camera-aware-fusion.json) leaves R016, R018, R022, and R024 as right-only reviews in its 2070–2160 and 2250–2340 s gaps. Their existing [source strips](right-entry-transfer/scotland-source-review/manifest.json) and [variant-addition strip](right-entry-transfer/scotland-variant-235-225-new-review/manifest.json) show short moving-stone paths.

A separate [postscore Scotland bridge replay](right-entry-transfer/scotland-1650-2760-bridged-camera-aware-fusion.json) makes all 11 right prefixes conditionally match left candidates. For the four formerly right-only paths, the bridged left IDs are R01-S56, R01-S59, R01-S61, and R01-S63, with image-event lags **7.708–9.871 s**. This supports review of the bridge's recovered coverage; it is not independent throw validation. The bridge has other known false motion candidates, and the right-view agreement for four selected paths does not validate every bridged event or justify silently promoting the gap as fully covered.
