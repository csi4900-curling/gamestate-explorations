# Source-derived right-house geometry and routed-view audit

The [fine-grid plan](SPLIT-HOUSE-ALIGNMENT-PLAN.md) was frozen after nine-frame development probing. Per-run source-frame temporal medians supply a painted-house image reference for the right panel. The fitted ellipses are **image geometry**, not rink-metres homographies: [Italy R01](../../canada-italy/right-house-reference-r01-development.json) and [R02](../../canada-italy/right-house-reference-r02-development.json) have centres near `(491,153)` and major/minor axes near `262×26` px; [Estonia R01](../../canada-estonia-2024/right-house-reference-r01-development.json) and [R02](../../canada-estonia-2024/right-house-reference-r02-development.json) have centres near `(478–479,162)` and axes near `274–278×26` px. The paint ellipse excludes the guard area and does not locate stone contact points on the ice.

At five-second source samples, the [Italy alignment audit](../../canada-italy/right-house-five-second-alignment-development.json) found **1,607/1,620** right-house-aligned samples and 13 low-overlap samples. The [Estonia audit](../../canada-estonia-2024/right-house-five-second-alignment-development.json) found **1,635/1,638** aligned and three low-overlap samples. These are sampled paint-visibility counts, not continuous camera-coverage percentages. The full [Italy low-sample source sheets](../../canada-italy/right-house-five-second-alignment-source-review/manifest.json) show that ten isolated or short R01 failures are players occluding the otherwise present house; two lie within five seconds of provisional left candidates, which **remain retained**. The [Estonia low-sample sheet](../../canada-estonia-2024/right-house-five-second-alignment-source-review/manifest.json) and Italy's final three low samples show closing credits, not rink views.

[Estonia source frames](../../canada-estonia-2024/right-house-terminal-cut-r02-source.jpg) bracket the same change between **8354.5 and 8355.5 s**. The [Italy terminal artifact](../../canada-italy/right-house-terminal-cut-r02-development.json) and [Estonia artifact](../../canada-estonia-2024/right-house-terminal-cut-r02-development.json) preserve each one-second paint score. The same per-run image ellipses also score the expanded-ROI cap observations by **distance from painted scoring house**, separately from ice context ([Italy](../../canada-italy/right-house-expanded-cap-image-geometry-development.json), [Estonia](../../canada-estonia-2024/right-house-expanded-cap-image-geometry-development.json)). This location cue cannot classify a stone or verify a physical board transition. Player occlusion can hide the house in valid split-view footage, and the sampling grid cannot certify every intervening frame or throw.
