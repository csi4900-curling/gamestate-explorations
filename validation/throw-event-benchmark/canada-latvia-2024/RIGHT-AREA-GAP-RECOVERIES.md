# Two source-visible right-camera paths omitted from the full-match handoff

The frozen [full-right area-aware scan](full-right-area-first-transfer.json) had already proposed two red moving paths that the earlier 147-candidate full-match handoff omitted. The omission was in the **candidate handoff**, not in that right-camera proposal generator. The handoff had preserved the older eight right-only entries in displayed end 5, then added only the 20 newer end-6 right-only reviews. It had not inspected the full-right scan's right-only proposals elsewhere in the match.

The [A008 audit](end1-omitted-right-area-source-audit/manifest.json) and [A367 audit](end5-omitted-right-area-source-audit/manifest.json) bind marked frames to the source video, frozen area scan, and previous ledger. Both classes were assigned by the same development agent **after** examining candidate gaps. The [expanded 149-candidate ledger](full-match-area-censored-candidate-handoff-two-area-gap-recoveries-development.json) inserts the two IDs without relabelling any earlier candidate. Its possible-event review count rises **96 → 98**. This count is not a physical throw count, precision, recall, or F1. Exact release, settle, complete board, metric trajectory, spin, and simulation readiness remain unknown for both additions.

The subsequent [complete right-only accounting](full-match-area-censored-candidate-handoff-all-area-right-only-accounted-development.json) reconciles **all 32** right-only proposals in the frozen full-right scan: 22 existing area rows (including A008/A367), seven time-and-colour pairs with older right entries, and [three source-reviewed nonstone abstentions](remaining-right-only-source-audit/manifest.json) for A042, A182, and A374. The seven pairs are **not** verified physical identity matches; their right-area source-pixel classes remain unreviewed. The older right entry R008 has no full-area match and remains in the ledger. Adding the three abstentions gives **152 total candidate rows** while leaving the possible-event review count at **98** and physical throw count unknown. The [reconciler](../../../prototype/full_right_only_reconciliation.py) rejects duplicate, missing, or unmatched frozen right-only proposals.

The broadcast display and source clearing show that the accepted yellow path S26 at 1057 s begins the next playing sequence; the person path S45 at 1882 s is an extra flag near the end of that sequence, not a missing end-2 delivery. On the current *development* grouping, end 1 now has 15 accepted left stone-body tracks plus A008, end 2 has 16 accepted left stone-body tracks plus person S45, ends 3 and 4 each have 16 accepted left stone-body tracks, end 5 has seven accepted left stone-body tracks, eight older right-only entries that still lack source-pixel classes, and A367, and end 6 has 16 source-reviewed right stone-pixel paths plus ambiguous left S119. These counts expose where review is needed; they do not prove one-to-one physical delivery identities or certify the game's shot sequence.

The broader [119-track left-crossing audit](ALL-LEFT-CROSSING-SOURCE-PIXEL-RESULTS.md) found no clearly stone-shaped track among 47 withheld left crossings. A008 demonstrates why that result cannot imply full-match recall: the missing stone was **never a left stone-body crossing track**. A right-only rescue channel must run across the whole routed match, including periods when the left panel looks active. It must keep source-pixel appearance and temporal evidence attached, and it must abstain on the [source-reviewed end-6 people/clothing paths](FULL-RIGHT-AREA-TRANSFER-RESULTS.md). The right camera may also miss paths; independent source-first labels remain necessary for event F1.
