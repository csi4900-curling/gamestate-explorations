# Canada–Latvia 2024: fixed-grid first transfer

This run follows the [pre-inference protocol](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/CANADA-LATVIA-2024-FIXED-GRID-TRANSFER-PLAN.md) on the fixed 1650–2760 s interval. The source-only full/left/right five-second sheets and six-checkpoint alignment audit were frozen before detector inference. There is **no independently labelled physical-throw reference** for this interval. The counts below describe proposals and abstentions; they are not precision, recall, or F1.

A separate [end-six split-panel registration transfer](SPLIT-PANEL-REGISTRATION-TRANSFER.md) applies the unchanged Czech camera-motion rule to Latvia source seconds 5670–6670. On selected source-reviewed image paths, it separates 40/40 moving-stone-pixel pairs above 5 px from 45/45 earlier quiet-body pairs below 5 px, while the one usable person/clothing path abstains. Those development samples are not an exhaustive physical-throw benchmark.

The unchanged 10 Hz [span detector](frozen-first-span-raw.json) produced **27** raw motion candidates. The fixed x = 160 ± 60 px [corridor](frozen-first-span-corridor.json) retained **19** and preserved eight as off-corridor candidates. The fixed-grid right-camera [selector](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-latvia-2024-fixed-grid-right-anchor-frozen-first.json) accepted only **1/111** original-reference frames and chose its sole accepted frame at 2605.0 s (13 feature inliers, 0.65 px feature RMSE). Most grid failures were excessive registration motion/error (89); the other 21 lacked spatial support. This weak one-frame anchor is an explicit reliability warning, not a reason to retune the frozen rule after viewing the result.

Despite that weak old-reference bridge, the selected local frame registered every candidate-local sample in the [motion scan](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-latvia-2024-fixed-grid-motion-merged-frozen-first.json): 168/168 left-view samples and 288/288 right-view samples. The scan selected **14** unique paths, found four without supported paths, and left one with ambiguous alternatives. The matching [same-shot hold](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-latvia-2024-fixed-grid-hold-merged-frozen-first.json) borrowed no transforms or added paths; bounded-gap selection also left the path counts unchanged. The frozen [three-point patch check](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-latvia-2024-fixed-grid-gap-patch-frozen-first.json) supported all 14 selected paths, and [timed-path duplicate checking](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-latvia-2024-fixed-grid-gap-dedup-frozen-first.json) found no pairs. The [final screen](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-latvia-2024-fixed-grid-gap-screen-frozen-first.json) therefore has **14 provisional motion-and-appearance candidates** and five abstentions: S05, S07, S14, S21, and S22. S21 has ambiguous motion paths; the other four lack a selected path.

A postscore [source-wide fixed-grid audit](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-latvia-2024-local-grid-coverage-development.json) used the *original 111 timestamps*, including the 110 that failed against the old reference. The selected local right-camera reference registered **111/111**, compared with the old reference's **1/111**. This is a stronger registration-coverage result than the candidate-only sample because its denominator was fixed independently of event proposals. It still samples only every ten seconds, is exposed development data, and does not measure physical throw recall or metric geometry. The [audit script](../../../prototype/scripts/audit_local_reference_grid_coverage.py) records source and code fingerprints.

A postscore [house projection overlay](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-latvia-2024-house-projection-development.jpg) visually aligns the transported button and three ring outlines with the painted house at the 2605 s anchor. It does **not** quantify landmark error, far-field stone position, or cross-camera identity. Local image registration and a plausible house overlay cannot confirm that the 14 proposals are deliveries, that all deliveries were found, or that a simulator can use the metric paths.

A later, exploratory [six-frame paint-alignment audit](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-latvia-2024-house-paint-alignment-development.json) checks the projected blue annulus against broad HSV-classified blue paint at fixed source times from 1855 to 2705 s. All six local registrations succeed. **75.5–84.8%** of classified blue pixels in the house ROI fall within the projection; intersection-over-union is **0.279–0.440**, versus at most **0.201** for the tested 100 px horizontal and 30 px vertical shifts. This supports gross house alignment across those frames. Occlusions, varying paint color, development-selected frames and mask, and the weak original-reference bridge prevent interpreting these scores as measured landmark or stone-position error. The [audit script](../../../prototype/scripts/audit_rebased_house_paint_alignment.py) makes the calculation reproducible; metric simulation readiness remains false.

The transfer result supports a narrow conclusion: the event-blind local-reference procedure can restore candidate-frame registration after a severe original-reference mismatch on a new recording, and the unchanged downstream chain yields bounded provisional event proposals. The next validation gate needs independent source-video throw labels; a separate geometry gate needs measured rink landmarks in multiple frames and views before metric trajectory or game-state simulation claims. Any analysis of the five abstentions or eight off-corridor candidates from this point is postscore development and must be kept separate from this first transfer.

The [artifact-chain verification](frozen-first-transfer-verification.json) checks source fingerprints, every stage link, candidate order, model fingerprint, and the final 27 → 19 → 14 accounting. It verifies reproducibility of the recorded run, not physical-event correctness.

## Postscore follow-up, separate from the frozen count

The [official results-book game page](https://curlit.com/PDF/WWhCC2024_ResultsBook.pdf?V=2) gives Latvia–Canada end scores for six completed ends: Latvia 0–1–0–1–0–0, Canada 2–0–3–0–2–2, final 2–9. The [local score context](../../official-shot-sequence/latvia-canada-2024-end-score-context.json) is checked against the PDF. It supplies end-level game-state constraints, **not** shot timestamps or throw labels; 96 deliveries is only the nominal six-end count and cannot be aligned to this video interval from the PDF alone.

One frozen abstention, S21, has a 15-sample observed shared motion prefix from 2360.5 to 2367.5 s, a conditional endpoint displacement of 13.17 m, and two accepted stone-patch samples. Its alternatives split at 2368.0 s. The [postscore prefix audit](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-latvia-2024-shared-prefix-proposals-development.json) preserves that evidence as **one partial review proposal** without asserting which later path is the stone or that it was a physical delivery. The source-only [right-camera sheet](source-only-right-5s-sheets/sheet-04.jpg) shows a red stone in transit near this period. This retrospective observation did not change the first-transfer 14-event screen. The same audit finds no qualifying shared-prefix proposal on the exposed Scotland development branch.

A [one-second right-view review strip](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-latvia-2024-abstention-review-development/manifest.json) separates the five abstentions into likely causes. S05/S07 coincide with red-clad players and sticks crossing an otherwise stone-free sampled right view. S14 is a red proposal while the already accepted S12 **yellow** stone is traveling and a red player moves in the background. S21/S22 overlap one visibly traveling **red** stone from roughly 2361–2368 s; the shared-prefix audit retains a path for S21, but the frozen screen accepts neither proposal. These are retrospective, non-independent frame judgments at one-second sampling. They prioritize a single lost physical episode for development; they do not revise the frozen count or establish precision/recall.

The separate [partial-episode union](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-latvia-2024-partial-episode-union-development.json) makes that review case machine-readable. It groups the S21 shared right-view prefix (15 observed samples, 2360.5–2367.5 s) with S22's red passage cue at 2363.742 s, which falls inside that interval and has no supported path of its own. S22's **left** receiving-view assignment differs from S21's **right** observed path; the union records that mismatch and leaves cross-camera physical identity unverified. The output preserves all 27 original candidate references, 14 frozen provisional events, five frozen abstentions, and eight off-corridor candidates. It adds **one partial review episode** containing two abstained candidates; S05/S07/S14 remain ungrouped abstentions. This is a postscore development proposal, not a fifteenth validated throw, a revised first-transfer score, or a simulation-ready path. The [union code](../../../prototype/partial_episode_union.py) and [exporter](../../../prototype/scripts/export_partial_episode_union.py) bind it to the frozen evidence hashes.

Separately, the **already existing** [conditional cap-dropout rule](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/LATVIA-2024-DROPOUT-POSTSCORE-PLAN.md), developed on earlier 2024 recordings, was applied unchanged to Latvia after the source had been inspected. Its [screen](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-latvia-2024-conditional-dropout-rescue-screen-postscore.json) preserves all 14 frozen accepted IDs and adds **S21 only**, with 15 shared-prefix motion samples and two of three accepted stone patches. The other four abstentions remain rejected. This shows a previously defined general rescue can surface the same visible red-stone episode; it does **not** revise the untouched Latvia first result or prove physical identity, recall, or full-path accuracy. The postscore S21/S22 episode union remains a separate review-level grouping.

A targeted [quiet-tail board scan](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-latvia-2024-partial-episode-quiet-board-development.json) samples 2368–2391 s after that partial episode using the same rebased camera and clean-background detector as the accepted-event board scan. All **24/24** sampled frames register; six stable stone proposals form a **partial** right-view board. The [neighbor comparison](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-latvia-2024-partial-episode-board-comparisons-development.json) abstains from comparing the preceding accepted S19 board because unassociated passage candidate S20 intervenes. It associates **five** same-colour nearby observations between the partial-episode board and the next accepted S23 board, with no intervening candidate after the grouped episode. Those associations are conditional view-local observations, not proven physical stone identities or evidence that any unmatched stone appeared or disappeared. Neither artifact supplies exact settle time, complete board state, or simulator-ready geometry.

An unchanged Czech-developed [board-blind cap-continuation transfer](CAP-CONTINUATION-TRANSFER.md) has now been run over the 14 frozen accepted Latvia candidates and their provisional partial boards. Twelve have a moving path and later same-colour board observation. Five cap paths abstain before the final onset; five cases have a cap endpoint within 1.1 source-image pixels of at least one board-frame detection, often a *later* observation rather than the first same-colour stone. Source packets for S16, S23 and S26 show moving yellow stones, but all physical identities and simulation flags remain unresolved. This previously exposed match provides a cross-recording development check, not independent throw F1 or settled-stone accuracy.

## Partial game-state handoff

A postscore [quiet-tail scan](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-latvia-2024-post-event-partial-boards-development.json) used the same recording-local registration and clean detection background as the throw pipeline. It found partial stable boards after **12 of the 14** provisional events. One event had a registered tail but no stable stone proposal; another had too little quiet time before the next candidate. The [adjacent-board ledger](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-latvia-2024-adjacent-partial-board-comparisons-development.json) permits seven same-view partial comparisons with 24 spatially associated observation pairs. It abstains once across a camera change, twice for missing boards, and three times because another passage candidate intervenes. These associations do not establish persistent physical stone identity or prove additions and removals.

The [event-to-state evidence handoff](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-latvia-2024-evidence-state-handoff-development.json) retains all 27 candidate records and attaches observed paths, partial boards, and the official end-score context where applicable. Every item leaves physical release time, end identity, complete board state, spin count, and simulation readiness unresolved. This is a concrete partial-state representation for later validation, not a finished game-state reconstruction or a coaching simulation.

An additive [event-level state timeline](../../curlobserver-learned-probe/canada-latvia-2024-episode-state-timeline-development.json) now joins the original handoff, the P01 partial episode, its six-stone partial board, and both neighbor comparisons. It preserves the 27-candidate ledger but exposes **15 event-level review proposals** in time order: 14 frozen unique-path proposals plus one lower-tier shared-prefix proposal between S19 and S23. S21/S22 map to that one P01 proposal and are not double-counted as separate event-level throws. The S20 abstention before P01 and five conditional observation pairs toward S23 remain attached. The schema keeps all 15 `simulation_ready` flags false and all exact release, settle, spin and end identities unknown. The [exporter](../../../prototype/scripts/export_episode_state_timeline.py) verifies the source and artifact chain.
