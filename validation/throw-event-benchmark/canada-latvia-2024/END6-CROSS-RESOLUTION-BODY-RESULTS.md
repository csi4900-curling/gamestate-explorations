# End-six 360p cap to 1080p body/handle correspondence

The 16 source-reviewed stone-pixel paths in the [right-view onset ledger](RIGHT-MOTION-ONSET-RESULTS.md) can be checked against existing, separately processed **resolution lanes of the same match**: the [1080p yellow body/handle paths](../layout-router/full-2024-coverage/latvia24-full-v1/end6-highres-body-handle-screen-development.json) and [1080p red body/handle paths](../layout-router/full-2024-coverage/latvia24-full-v1/end6-highres-red-full-transfer-development.json). Both high-resolution artifacts fingerprint the same 5670–6670 s video excerpt. The [reproducible association](end6-cross-resolution-body-cap-development.json) compares only synchronous moving frames of the same colour, requiring at least two common seconds, at most 12 px horizontal difference at 360p scale, and a body center 0–16 px below the cap center. This rule was chosen after inspecting the Latvia offsets; it is a development cross-check, not a blind validation gate.

**All 16** reviewed moving-stone-pixel paths have exactly one compatible high-resolution body/handle path. The four reviewed people/clothing paths were deliberately not used as stone-path association targets. Across **56 synchronous frame pairs**, the median absolute horizontal difference is **0.75 px** at 360p scale (maximum 4.83 px). The high-resolution body center sits a median **8.33 px below** the low-resolution coloured cap center (range 5.67–9.67 px). Eight red and eight yellow paths are linked; three other red high-resolution paths remain unassigned and unclassified by this comparison.

This supports **observed-frame stone-body localization** for the 16 selected cap paths. It does not establish that an earlier stationary cap and the later moving cap are the same physical stone. The high-resolution tracks extend the low-resolution moving observation by only **one second in six paths**, and not at all in the other ten. Both views therefore lose the traveling stone before its physical settle. No complete trajectory, cross-camera identity, metric rink position, complete board, spin count, or simulator-ready state follows from this association. The red and yellow templates and the source-frame classes are same-match development evidence, not independent full-interval throw labels.

A later [field-of-view audit](END6-FIELD-OF-VIEW-RESULTS.md) identifies the original 1080p search window's y=970 cutoff as one reason paths stop early. A selected yellow stone remains visible beyond that cutoff and then exits the actual frame at y=1080; the final observation is not a settle.
