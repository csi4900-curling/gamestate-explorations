# Board-blind cap continuation: Latvia development transfer

The [reproducible transfer artifact](board-blind-cap-continuation-transfer-development.json) applies the *unchanged Czech-developed* red/yellow HSV component masks and causal pixel-motion association rule to the Canada–Latvia 2024 source interval at 1920 × 1080, source seconds 1650–2760. The rule starts from the last two positions of each existing provisional moving path, samples every 0.5 s, chooses using only past path positions and the current source frame, and abstains when no component or competing components satisfy the fixed gate. Later partial-board observation times bound the probe; their positions never select a cap. Source, motion, board, code and output lineage are pinned by SHA-256.

All **14** frozen accepted candidates are conserved. Twelve have at least two moving-path points and a later same-colour stable partial-board observation; two lack such a board observation. Of the 12 eligible candidates, **five abstain** before their last same-colour board onset (S15, S18, S19, S25 and S27). Seven reach their final onset, but endpoint agreement varies widely. **Five** candidates have at least one cap endpoint within **1.1 image pixels** of a same-colour local board-frame detection: S01, S04, S16, S23 and S26. S02 and S03 reach their only same-colour onset but remain **309.357** and **212.499 pixels** away, respectively. These are related-pixel comparisons, not accuracy scores or verified stone identities.

Board-observation order matters. S04, S16, S23 and S26 are far from an earlier same-colour board observation but close to a later one. The [source packet](board-blind-cap-continuation-source-probe/manifest.json) shows visibly moving yellow stones for [S16](board-blind-cap-continuation-source-probe/s16-cap-continuation.jpg), [S23](board-blind-cap-continuation-source-probe/s23-cap-continuation.jpg) and [S26](board-blind-cap-continuation-source-probe/s26-cap-continuation.jpg). S04 has numerical proximity in the artifact but has not received the same source-sheet review. Source review is same-agent, after seeing transfer output, and cannot validate the model's event precision or persistent identity.

This recording was previously exposed to detector development, and Czechia and Latvia share a 2024 broadcast style. Thus this is a **cross-recording development diagnostic**, not an untouched external validation. The underlying candidate paths and boards are provisional. A close cap/board pixel center can reflect related detection stages; a large separation can mean that the first board stone was already present or the moving stone has not yet stabilized. All 14 physical identities remain unresolved, including the five with close endpoints. No throw F1, complete game state, metric position error or simulation-ready event follows from this result.
