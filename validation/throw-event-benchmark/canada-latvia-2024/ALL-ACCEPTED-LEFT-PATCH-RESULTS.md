# Frozen patch appearance on all 72 accepted Latvia left crossings

The [complete accepted-crossing selection](all-accepted-left-crossing-patch-selection.json) replays every one of the 72 left passage candidates in the saved Latvia timeline. It captures three crossing-local tracked-object crops per row (−0.2 s, crossing, +0.2 s) in the [source-linked packet](all-accepted-left-crossing-patch-packet/manifest.json), then applies the [unchanged China-trained crossing-patch model](../../official-shot-sequence/identity-crossing-transfer-v1/model.npz). The [full score file](all-accepted-left-crossing-patch-score-development.json) contains every candidate's three scores and mean. The packet checks that the 360p video bytes match the timeline source; crop, model, feature-code and runner hashes are recorded.

The model marks **38/72** accepted passages as stone-like and **34/72** negative. Among the 13 previously source-reviewed clear paths, it scores **10/12** visible stone-body paths positive and **1/1** person path negative. One previously reviewed ambiguous candidate is also negative. Of the remaining **58 unreviewed** rows, 28 score positive and 30 negative. These numbers describe model outputs, **not throw precision or recall**.

The [exact-source review of the five lowest-scoring unreviewed rows](lowest-five-patch-source-audit/lowest-five-source-frames.jpg) checks S15, S05, S105, S13 and S54. All five marked short paths show a yellow cap with a stone body moving across three frames, often partly obscured by players. Their model scores range from **−2.632 to −1.997**. The [review manifest](lowest-five-patch-source-audit/manifest.json) records source times, marked pixels, scores, hashes and the same-agent postscore provenance. These five are selected *because* their scores were lowest, so they cannot estimate overall stone retention. They do prove that the negative extreme is not a safe nonstone class, nor automatically a good false-positive review queue.

The zero-score cutoff would discard at least the two earlier reviewed Latvia stone paths plus these five additional visible stone paths. None is independently certified as a complete physical delivery, so this is a **source-stone-path retention failure**, not a measured throw-event false-negative count. A patch score remains graded object evidence; detector and game-state decisions stay unchanged.

The [all-left enriched full-match ledger](full-match-area-censored-candidate-handoff-all-left-patch-evidence-development.json) attaches these scores to **all 72** accepted left rows within the existing 147-row candidate inventory. It keeps unreviewed source status explicitly unknown, the possible-event review count at 96, the verified physical throw count unknown, and every simulation-readiness flag false. The earlier 13-review enrichment remains an immutable narrower evidence snapshot.

The [later source-review-enriched ledger](full-match-area-censored-candidate-handoff-all-left-patch-and-source-development.json) additionally attaches the five low-score source-pixel observations. Each row retains the fact that it was **unreviewed at scoring time** and marks the review as added afterward. This preserves the frozen score and its selection history while making the latest pixel evidence available to the game-state handoff. It still does not promote any row to a verified physical throw.

A later [complete negative-tier review](ALL-NEGATIVE-PATCH-SOURCE-RESULTS.md) inspected all 34 negative rows: 32 have visible stone-body pixels in the crossing crops, one is a person and one remains ambiguous. Its [intermediate full-match ledger](full-match-area-censored-candidate-handoff-all-negative-reviewed-development.json) extends the five-case review handoff; the frozen model scores and event decisions are unchanged.

The [positive tier was then completely reviewed](ALL-ACCEPTED-LEFT-SOURCE-PIXEL-RESULTS.md), yielding a source-pixel class for all 72 accepted left crossings. The later [all-accepted source-review ledger](full-match-area-censored-candidate-handoff-all-accepted-left-source-reviewed-development.json) supersedes the negative-only ledger as the most complete left-crossing evidence handoff. It still does not verify 72 physical throws.
