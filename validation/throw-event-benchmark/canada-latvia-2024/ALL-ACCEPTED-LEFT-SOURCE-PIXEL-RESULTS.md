# Source-pixel review of every accepted Latvia left crossing

The saved left-camera detector has **72 accepted passage candidates** across its routed full-match interval. Every one now has three crossing-local, source-linked object crops in the [packet](all-accepted-left-crossing-patch-packet/manifest.json). The complete postscore review covers both the [34 negative-score rows](ALL-NEGATIVE-PATCH-SOURCE-RESULTS.md) and all [38 positive-score rows](all-positive-patch-source-audit/manifest.json). The positive review is inspectable on [pages 1](all-positive-patch-source-audit/positive-page-1.jpg), [2](all-positive-patch-source-audit/positive-page-2.jpg), [3](all-positive-patch-source-audit/positive-page-3.jpg), [4](all-positive-patch-source-audit/positive-page-4.jpg), and [5](all-positive-patch-source-audit/positive-page-5.jpg).

Across **all 72 accepted left crossings**, the marked crops show **70 stone-body paths**, **one person-head path (S45)**, and **one ambiguous yellow path near a player (S119)**. The 38 positive-score rows all show stone bodies; the 34 negative-score rows contain 32 stone bodies, S45 and S119. These classes are *same-agent source-pixel judgments made after seeing detector proposals and scores*. They describe what appears near the tracked crossing, **not** whether each row is a distinct delivered stone. A short path can switch identity, duplicate another event, or belong to an older stone; source coverage also misses some possible throws. Therefore **70/72 is not event precision**, and there is no independent full-match recall or F1 measurement.

The [current full-match evidence handoff](full-match-area-censored-candidate-handoff-all-accepted-left-source-reviewed-development.json) attaches those 72 source-pixel classes and all 72 frozen patch scores to the existing 147 candidate rows. It preserves prior reviews, records postscore review provenance, retains the 96 *review-tier* possible-event proposals, leaves the verified physical throw count unknown, and keeps every state non-simulation-ready. The [positive-tier renderer](../../../prototype/scripts/audit_latvia_all_positive_patches.py) checks crop and video fingerprints; the [exporter](../../../prototype/scripts/export_latvia_all_accepted_source_pixels.py) checks score, packet and review-page hashes before joining.

This result shifts the next detector work toward **recall and physical-event identity**: restore throws in routed or static-left gaps, link an observed stone across camera changes without mistaking nearby people or older stones for the delivery, and deduplicate repeated passages. A hard negative patch-score veto would instead discard many of the already visible stone paths. Complete independent end-level throw labels remain necessary before claiming high precision, recall or F1. A separate state stage still needs persistent stone identities and verified settled boards before physics replay or coaching conclusions.
