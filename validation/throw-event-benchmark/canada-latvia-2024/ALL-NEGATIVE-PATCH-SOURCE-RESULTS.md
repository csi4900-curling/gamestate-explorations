# All 34 negative patch scores, checked against crossing crops

The [frozen all-left score](all-accepted-left-crossing-patch-score-development.json) marks 34 of Latvia's 72 accepted left passages negative. The [complete postscore source-pixel review](all-negative-patch-source-audit/manifest.json) checks the three source-linked crossing crops for **every one of those 34**. The four [review pages](all-negative-patch-source-audit/negative-page-1.jpg) ([2](all-negative-patch-source-audit/negative-page-2.jpg), [3](all-negative-patch-source-audit/negative-page-3.jpg), [4](all-negative-patch-source-audit/negative-page-4.jpg)) show the objects without hiding low-score failures.

At the marked crossings, **32/34** negative rows visibly contain stone-body pixels, **S45** contains person-head pixels, and **S119** remains ambiguous yellow pixels near a player. Many low scores occur where a stone is partly occluded by a player; several unobstructed stones also score negative. Of the 36 yellow and 36 red accepted passages, **24 yellow** and **10 red** have negative scores. That is an association in this recording, not proof that the model has a general colour bias; colour, view direction, player position and background are confounded here.

This is a deliberately score-selected, same-agent review. A visible stone in a 48-pixel crossing crop does **not** prove a newly delivered physical stone, a continuous path, or a correct event time. Still, the result directly rules out a zero-score hard nonstone veto for this candidate set: it would reject 32 rows with visible stone bodies. The negative tier is not even a reliable queue of false event flags. The 38 positive rows have not received the same exhaustive source review, and no full-match throw precision, recall or F1 is inferred.

The [latest full-match evidence ledger](full-match-area-censored-candidate-handoff-all-negative-reviewed-development.json) attaches all 72 frozen scores and all 34 postscore crop reviews, preserving prior S45 and S119 reviews and marking newer labels as added after scoring. It retains all 147 candidate rows, the 96 review-tier possible-event count, an unknown verified physical throw count and false simulation readiness throughout. The [renderer](../../../prototype/scripts/audit_latvia_all_negative_patches.py) verifies source, packet and crop hashes and saves page hashes; the [exporter](../../../prototype/scripts/export_latvia_crossing_patch_candidate_enrichment.py) checks score, model, review-page and source lineage before attaching evidence.
