# Frozen larger-cap right-view transfer on Canada–Italy 2024

The [predeclared plan](RIGHT-LARGE-CAP-TRANSFER-PLAN.md) fixed the post-Estonia 180 px² cap ceiling, global assignment, late seed, 1650–2760 s interval and paired 0.25 context thresholds before this Italy run. Italy had prior development exposure; this is an unchanged-rule **same-family transfer**, not independent physical-delivery validation.

The [fixed probe](right-large-cap-fixed-transfer.json) emits 33 raw tracks and **19 directional review paths**. All 19 were rendered in four [source sheets](right-large-cap-source-review/manifest.json). [Source-pixel classes](right-large-cap-source-review/source-prefix-classes.json) were saved before appearance scores: 15 short moving-stone paths, two long paths containing stone pixels with uncertain same-colour identity, and two person/wheelchair paths.

The [paired context result](right-large-cap-combined-context-transfer.json) flags both person paths, including one that the surrounding-ring cue alone left clear, and retains all 15 short stone paths in clear review. The [reproducible passage audit](right-large-cap-coarse-overlap-transfer.json) finds a same-colour reviewed stone-pixel proposal in each of the **17 earlier same-agent coarse windows**, one proposal per window. I01 and I02 remain long identity-uncertain paths. The previous 120 px² [global-assignment transfer](RIGHT-GLOBAL-ASSIGNMENT-TRANSFER-RESULTS.md) also overlapped all 17 windows and produced 19 review paths; the larger cap ceiling did not create a new Italy coverage gain. It shifted two passage times modestly.

Estonia's gain was therefore recovery of a specifically oversized visible yellow cap, with no observed loss in this Italy transfer. The sample does **not** establish cross-recording robustness, event precision/recall/F1, exact segment endpoints, cross-view identity, or settled board states. The 2025 reserved end has not been used.

The [full-timeline evidence overlay](right-large-cap-full-timeline-overlay.json) retains all 19 right paths beside the unchanged 109-event provisional match timeline. Fifteen short source-reviewed stone paths have exactly one same-colour left event 0–20 seconds earlier. The two long identity-uncertain paths and two person paths remain review items; the first two left events in this interval lack a short right link. The links are conditional temporal/colour corroboration, not verified physical stone identity or additional accepted throws.

## Exposed moving-tail replay

The [pre-recorded plan](RIGHT-MOVING-TAIL-TRANSFER-PLAN.md) documents that the moving-tail rule was devised after inspecting the long-track timing profiles in **both** recordings. Applied unchanged to Italy's [larger-cap probe](right-large-cap-fixed-transfer.json), the [tail derivative](right-large-cap-motion-tail-fixed-transfer.json) shortens the two 20 s stationary-plus-moving paths A001/A005 and preserves the other 17 proposals. Both changed tails visibly follow stones in the new [source sheet](right-large-cap-motion-tail-source-review/sheet-01.jpg); the remaining sheets are byte-identical to the prior review. The [source-pixel review](right-large-cap-motion-tail-source-review/source-prefix-classes.json) was saved before context scoring.

The [paired context diagnostic](right-large-cap-motion-tail-combined-context-transfer.json) keeps 17/17 short stone tails clear and flags both person paths. The [coarse-window audit](right-large-cap-motion-tail-coarse-overlap-transfer.json) remains 17/17. A direction-aware [timeline overlay](right-large-cap-motion-tail-timeline-overlay.json) conditionally pairs all 17 short stone tails with unique same-colour provisional left events; the first two positive-y right passages precede their left passages by about 7.4 and 3.1 s. This is **not independent validation** or evidence that the stationary and moving portions were one physical stone. No event count, release/settle timestamp or game state was promoted.
