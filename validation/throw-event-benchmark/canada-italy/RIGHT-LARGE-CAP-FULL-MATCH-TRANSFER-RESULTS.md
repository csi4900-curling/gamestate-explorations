# Right-view full-match transfer: Canada–Italy 2024

The [predeclared plan](RIGHT-LARGE-CAP-FULL-MATCH-TRANSFER-PLAN.md) carried the Estonia larger-cap, moving-tail, temporal, and context rules unchanged into Italy's routed split-view runs. This is an **exposed-recording development transfer**: part of Italy was seen during rule development. It is not an independent event-accuracy benchmark.

The unchanged [left timeline](../layout-router/full-2024-coverage/new-preflight-italy24/timeline.json) has **109 provisional events**. The right scan produced [135 R01 proposals](right-large-cap-full-r01-tail.json) and [zero R02 proposals](right-large-cap-full-r02-tail.json). R01 covers `[90,8100)` seconds and R02 `[8190,8280)`; the other **240 of 8340 seconds** are explicit layout gaps. Forty long R01 tracks had their stationary-plus-moving prefixes shortened. One long path had no supported short tail. These are right **paths**, not 135 distinct deliveries.

The [time/colour inventory](right-large-cap-full-supported-temporal-inventory.json) preserves every candidate. It has **101** one-to-one temporal matches, **four** right paths competing for **two** left events, **30** right-only paths, and **six** left-only events. A time/colour match is a candidate association, not verified cross-camera stone identity.

All 34 right-side disagreements were rendered from source frames ([R01 sheets](right-large-cap-full-r01-disagreement-source-review/manifest.json), [R02 review](right-large-cap-full-r02-disagreement-source-review/source-prefix-classes.json)) and [classified by the same developer](right-large-cap-full-supported-review-audit.json):

| Right path group | Moving-stone pixels | Person/equipment pixels |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 30 right-only paths | 8 | 22 |
| 4 paths competing for 2 left events | 2 | 2 |

The eight right-only moving-stone paths are possible left-detector misses or timing/association failures. The two duplicate moving-stone paths show that a path count must not be promoted to a throw count. The [unreviewed context sweep](right-large-cap-full-supported-unreviewed-context.json) marked 20/22 right-only person paths cluttered, but left **two person paths clear** and flagged **one of eight moving-stone paths** as cluttered. It flagged both competing person paths and kept both competing moving-stone paths clear. The sole time-matched, cluttered path [visibly follows a moving stone near players](right-large-cap-full-r01-matched-cluttered-source-review/source-prefix-classes.json), another reason not to turn this cue into a hard veto. A preregistered time-spread [12-of-100 matched-clear sample](right-large-cap-full-matched-clear-source-sample/selection.json) showed moving stone pixels in all 12 [source reviews](right-large-cap-full-matched-clear-source-sample/source-prefix-classes.json); it does not estimate precision for the other 88.

All six left-only event pixels show moving stones in the [source review](right-large-cap-full-left-only-source-review/source-prefix-classes.json). This is materially different from Estonia, where the four left-only paths appeared to follow people or equipment. Right-camera [context frames](right-large-cap-full-left-only-right-context/manifest.json) show visible stone pixels for several Italy misses; sparse stills cannot establish visibility at every instant. The standard right rule misses these six for distinct observed reasons: short motion below its 60 px span threshold (S16, S27, S77), small early cap areas below its 12 px² seed threshold (S66, S79), or no nearby same-colour raw track (S153). Some long tracks also blend stationary and moving pixels, so their identity remains uncertain.

An explicitly **post-review, left-only-targeted** [relaxed raw-motion probe](right-large-cap-full-left-only-relaxed-raw-motion.json) searches the existing 1 Hz raw tracks for at least 30 px in at most 5 s, an initial cap of at least 5 px², and three observations. It finds one or more same-colour right raw paths near **five of six** Italy left-only events, with two paths for S16. [Source sheets](right-large-cap-full-left-only-relaxed-source-review/manifest.json) show moving stone pixels on five of those six paths; A166 includes old stationary yellow pixels before later moving yellow, so physical identity is uncertain. The [paired context cue](right-large-cap-full-left-only-relaxed-combined-context.json) marks four of five moving-stone paths clear, one cluttered, and the uncertain A166 path cluttered. The same targeted probe yielded **zero** raw candidates for the four likely false Estonia left-only paths ([artifact](../canada-estonia-2024/right-large-cap-full-left-only-relaxed-raw-motion.json)). This is a useful failure diagnostic, **not** a validated rescue rule: it depends on an existing left proposal, was devised after seeing Italy misses, and cannot recover a throw missed by both cameras.

The current output remains a provisional candidate inventory. No independent exhaustive physical throw labels, full-video precision/recall/F1, exact release and settle times, verified cross-view identity, or post-throw game states follow from these results. The reserved unseen 2025 end remains untouched.

The [ordered candidate evidence sequence](right-large-cap-full-candidate-evidence-sequence-v2.json) now provides a reproducible handoff: **139 rows** comprise all 109 left proposals plus 30 right-only proposals, including source-reviewed people rather than silently discarding them. It retains all 135 right paths in a separate table, competing temporal links, context tiers, six left-only source classes, and the targeted raw-path evidence for five Italy left-only events. Each row explicitly leaves release, settle, complete board, persistent identity, metric trajectory, spin, and simulation readiness unknown. This is a machine-readable *candidate sequence*, not a 139-throw count.

The established 2024 split-view right-panel cap sampler was then applied **after every Italy left candidate** at passage +15, +20, and +25 s, provided the three frames stayed inside its routed run and ended at least 5 s before the next provisional left event. The [source-frame scan](right-large-cap-full-post-left-partial-caps.json) has 73/109 rows with at least one stable coloured cap proposal (83 cap proposals total), 24 with none, 11 scene/player-motion abstentions, and one next-event abstention. A [time-spread eight-row source sheet](right-large-cap-post-left-partial-cap-sample/sheet.jpg) shows the right house, visible stones, and frequent player/wheelchair overlap at sampled times. This is a source sanity check, not a precision sample. The [joined partial-state handoff](right-large-cap-full-candidate-partial-state-sequence.json) attaches each scan result to its candidate row while keeping the complete board, physical settle, and simulation fields unknown. The cap ROI is only a partial right-camera view; stable colour blobs may also be nonstones.

A fixed [pre-candidate scan](right-large-cap-full-pre-left-partial-caps.json) samples passage −25, −20 and −15 s with previous-event and run guards. It finds 81 rows with stable caps, 22 with none, five scene/player-motion abstentions and one previous-event abstention. Comparing [pre/post image caps](right-large-cap-full-pre-post-partial-cap-deltas.json) with a 20-intensity-unit cross-window scene guard yields **88 comparable image windows**, 17 window abstentions and four cross-window scene abstentions. Within comparable windows, 49 local same-colour positions persist, 25 caps appear and 35 disappear; 43 rows have at least one appearance or disappearance. The [six-row source sheet](right-large-cap-pre-post-delta-source-sample/sheet.jpg), selected by time before viewing, shows that several apparent changes occur with players directly over the house or holding stones. The [per-candidate pre/post handoff](right-large-cap-full-candidate-pre-post-image-state-handoff.json) therefore records **visible cap changes**, not physical additions, removals, settled boards, or scoring transitions.

The unchanged moving-path ice-context cue was applied to every stable pre/post cap ([scores](right-large-cap-full-pre-post-cap-context.json)); it places 12 image-change rows in the clear tier and 31 in the cluttered tier ([triage](right-large-cap-full-pre-post-cap-delta-context-triage.json)). Source review found four *clear-tier* false cap changes caused by the ROI edge. The [expanded-ROI diagnostic and Estonia transfer](EXPANDED-BOARD-CAP-ROI-RESULTS.md) retains that visibly persistent stone but also produces many more cluttered proposals. No cap-change tier is promoted to a physical game-state transition.
