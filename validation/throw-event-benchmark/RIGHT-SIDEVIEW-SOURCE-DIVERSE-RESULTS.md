# Source-diverse side-view patch development

The [fixed training selection](right-sideview-source-diverse-development-v1/selection.json) uses **no Latvia video or labels**. It contains 114 previously source-reviewed right-camera paths from Italy, Estonia and USA 2024: 59 with moving-stone pixels and 55 with person/equipment pixels. Nine uncertain, mixed or person-handled-stone paths are excluded from binary fitting. The three source frames and original tracked cap boxes for every selected path are recorded with video, scan and review hashes. Ten repeated crops were deduplicated, leaving 332 unique training patches. All labels are same-agent short-prefix source judgments, not physical throw ground truth.

The [development model](right-sideview-source-diverse-development-v1/model.npz) retains the earlier 48-pixel-minimum cap-context crop, HOG/HSV descriptor, standardized balanced linear SVM (`C=0.1`) and zero decision threshold. In [leave-one-match-out checks](right-sideview-source-diverse-development-v1/training-results.json), at least one of three patches is positive on **59/59 reviewed moving-stone paths**. Two of 55 reviewed person/equipment paths also have a positive patch (one in the Estonia fold, one in USA). The folds hold out entire video matches, but the source reviews are selected, correlated and share broadcast conventions.

The model was then applied unchanged to the [same 53 Latvia source-reviewed prefixes](right-sideview-source-diverse-development-v1/latvia-fixed-source-review-transfer.json) previously scored by the frozen 2022-trained side-view model. At least one patch is positive on **18/18 stone-pixel paths** and **0/23 person/equipment paths**; seven of 12 mixed/identity-uncertain paths also have a positive patch. Seventeen stone paths have three positive frames and one has two. The older model had at least one positive patch on eight of 18 stone paths and six of 23 person/equipment paths. This is a useful appearance-transfer improvement on these source pixels. The old model's Latvia misses were already inspected when the new training design was chosen, so the Latvia comparison is **development transfer, not a sealed held-out score**. Duplicate proposals for the same image path also make these counts correlated.

A positive patch is only evidence of stone-like pixels. It can come from a handled or displaced stone; it does not identify a newly delivered stone, pair cameras, locate release or settle, count physical throws, recover a complete board, or establish full-match precision/recall/F1. A negative patch cannot safely reject an unreviewed throw until an exhaustive independent event benchmark establishes recall. The next step is source review of predictions outside the selected 53 paths.

The [full Latvia score](canada-latvia-2024/full-right-all-proposal-source-diverse-patch-development.json) now preserves all 336 original proposal IDs and reproduces the 53 fixed reviewed scores exactly. The new model marks 275 proposals stone-like; the older model marked 141. Relative to the old model, 147 proposals turn positive and 13 turn negative. Eleven of the 147 gains have source-pixel review (ten stone, one mixed); **136 gains are unreviewed**. Eight of the 13 losses have review (six person/equipment, two mixed); **five losses are unreviewed**. Under the unchanged paired context cue, 90 of 118 review groups contain a proposal with both visual cues positive, nine contain only cue disagreement, and 19 have both cues weak on every member. These are review priorities, not 90 or 275 physical throws. The unreviewed gains and losses must be inspected before promoting this model into an automatic event filter.

A [fixed source-frame flip packet](canada-latvia-2024/full-right-patch-flip-source-probe/manifest.json) and its [same-agent pixel audit](canada-latvia-2024/full-right-patch-flip-source-audit.json) now inspect all five previously unreviewed losses plus 15 newly positive proposals selected across time from groups without previous source reviews. **All 15 sampled gains show moving-stone pixels** in their highlighted three-frame prefixes. Of the five losses, **four show person or wheelchair pixels** and **one is mixed**: early samples contain a partly obscured stone, while the final sample lands on a person. This supports the model's local appearance ranking on this deliberately selected packet; it does not score the other 121 unreviewed gains, establish complete-track identity, or measure physical throw recall. The mixed loss must remain in review, and no negative model score is a safe automatic event veto.

Reproduce the training and fixed reviewed transfer with `feasibility/scripts/python.sh prototype/scripts/select_2024_right_patch_training.py`, `feasibility/scripts/python.sh prototype/scripts/train_2024_right_patch_from_source_reviews.py`, and `feasibility/scripts/python.sh prototype/scripts/transfer_2024_right_patch_to_latvia.py` from the repository root. For the full match, run `feasibility/scripts/python.sh prototype/scripts/score_latvia_full_right_source_diverse_patch.py --shard-index N` for each `N` in `0, 1, 2, 3`, then `feasibility/scripts/python.sh prototype/scripts/combine_latvia_source_diverse_patch_shards.py`. The four source-verified shards and merged output preserve input hashes and model provenance. The fixed flip packet is generated by `prototype/scripts/build_latvia_patch_flip_source_probe.py`; rerun its audit with `python prototype/scripts/audit_latvia_patch_flip_source_probe.py` to check source-page and input hashes.
