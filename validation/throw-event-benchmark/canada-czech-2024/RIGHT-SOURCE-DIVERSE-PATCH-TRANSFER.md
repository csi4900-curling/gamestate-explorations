# Frozen source-diverse patch transfer on Canada–Czechia

The [full right-proposal score](full-right-source-diverse-patch-transfer-development.json) applies the unchanged 2024 side-view patch model, trained on Italy, Estonia and USA source-pixel reviews, to all **131** proposals in the existing Canada–Czechia right-camera moving-tail inventory. The scorer samples the first, middle and last tracked prefix points (two points for the one shorter prefix), preserving every proposal ID. **94/131** have at least one positive patch: 92 have three, two have two, and 37 have none. This is a cross-recording appearance transfer, **not** a physical throw count or event-level precision/recall result.

The two previously [source-reviewed person false positives](full-corridor-right-only-top-patch-review/source-review.json), R01:A035 and R01:A100, now each score negative on all three sampled patches. Both had scored three positive patches under the earlier 2022-trained model at its different sample times. This supports a local improvement on those two selected controls, but does not establish performance on other people or stones in the Czech source.

The [episode inventory](full-corridor-right-episodes-first-transfer.json) contains 23 right-only proposals. Under the new model, 22 have zero positive sampled patches; one, R01:A191, has two positives. A [fixed packet of the scored source frames](full-right-only-source-diverse-review-packet/manifest.json) and [same-agent pixel audit](full-right-only-source-diverse-pixel-audit.json) now cover **all 23**. Every highlighted prefix follows person or wheelchair pixels. The 22 negative model results agree with those sampled classes; the one positive, R01:A191, follows a player's Canada shirt and is an appearance false positive. The two previously reviewed controls, A035 and A100, are among the 22 negatives. This is selected postscore source-pixel review, not a blind event benchmark. A person-prefix track cannot verify whether a separate real stone was missed elsewhere in its time window. None of the 23 IDs is deleted from the episode inventory.

A second [fixed packet](full-linked-right-patch-disagreement-source-packet/manifest.json) covers every **left-linked** right proposal with zero positive patches, plus the remaining positive proposal in an ambiguous multi-right episode: 16 prefixes across six episodes. Its [source-pixel audit](full-linked-right-patch-disagreement-pixel-audit.json) finds **15 negative person/equipment prefixes and one positive moving-stone prefix**. In episode E022, R01:A029 follows a red stone while nearby R01:A030, A034 and A036 follow an upright implement or people; the patch cue separates these sampled objects despite their temporal compatibility. The other four multi-right episodes have only person/equipment prefixes in this selected set, and the sole negative one-to-one match, R01:A376, follows a person's clothing. This does not prove the four episodes lack a separate untracked stone, nor does it validate the remaining 90 positive one-to-one proposals. A left/right time match is not verified physical identity.

A third [fixed packet](full-linked-positive-patch-source-probe/manifest.json) samples **20 of those 90 positive one-to-one proposals**: 12 spread over the ordered match timeline and eight remaining proposals with the lowest mean three-frame patch score. Its [same-agent source-pixel audit](full-linked-positive-patch-pixel-audit.json) finds moving-stone pixels in all 20 highlighted prefixes, including all eight lower-score selections. The result supports appearance ranking across this Czech recording, but 70 positive one-to-one proposals remain unsampled. The selected set is not an unbiased precision estimate; source pixels and time compatibility still do not verify a physical delivery or complete cross-camera stone identity.

Reproduce with `feasibility/scripts/python.sh prototype/scripts/score_czech_full_right_source_diverse_patch.py` after moving the existing output aside. The right-only source packet is generated by `feasibility/scripts/python.sh prototype/scripts/build_czech_right_only_patch_source_packet.py` and its frozen image/input hashes are checked by `python prototype/scripts/audit_czech_right_only_patch_source_packet.py`. Generate and check the linked-disagreement packet with `feasibility/scripts/python.sh prototype/scripts/build_czech_linked_patch_disagreement_packet.py` and `python prototype/scripts/audit_czech_linked_patch_disagreement_packet.py`. Generate and check the positive one-to-one packet with `feasibility/scripts/python.sh prototype/scripts/build_czech_linked_positive_patch_probe.py` and `python prototype/scripts/audit_czech_linked_positive_patch_probe.py`. The artifacts record source, model, training selection, scan and code hashes. The model was frozen before this Czech scoring run, but the project had already inspected parts of this match during detector development; this is not a sealed independent holdout. Complete-match segmentation F1 and settled game states remain unknown.
