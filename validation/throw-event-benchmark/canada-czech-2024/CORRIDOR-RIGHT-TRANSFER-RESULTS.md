# Canada–Czechia corridor/right-camera candidate coverage

This is a **prediction-only** transfer, not a throw-event accuracy measurement. The [prerun plan](CORRIDOR-RIGHT-TRANSFER-PLAN.json) fixed source and code hashes, the unchanged 1 Hz right-cap tracker, 20-second direction-aware join, frozen patch model, and a scan margin of 25 seconds around the 1650–2760 s candidate interval. The margin matters because the first left candidate is at 1650.65 s and its matching right passage precedes it. No independent timestamped throw reference exists for this interval.

The [right scan](corridor-right-probe-first-transfer.json) produced 23 directional proposals. Its [moving-tail derivative](corridor-right-tail-first-transfer.json) preserved all 23. The [join](corridor-right-corrob-first-transfer.json) found a compatible right proposal for **all 19** frozen left corridor candidates: 14 had a unique one-to-one temporal match and five were ambiguous. This is candidate coverage, **not 19 confirmed deliveries**. The [frozen patch features](corridor-right-patch-evidence-first-transfer.json) scored 19 matched right tracks; 13 of the 14 right tracks uniquely linked to a left candidate were positive in all three sampled frames, while S02's track was positive in two.

The [cross-view episode inventory](corridor-right-episodes-first-transfer.json) preserves all 19 left IDs and all 23 right proposal IDs in 20 review episodes: 14 one-to-one, one shared-right group, one multi-right group, and four right-only proposals. The multi-right group contains S04–S06 at 1903–1907 s; a separate same-agent [source review](POSTSCORE-END-RESET-REVIEW.md) places these left passages during an end reset. The shared-right group contains S12 and S13, agreeing with the earlier frozen screen's duplicate-path relationship. These agreements are useful error triage, not independent throw labels. The four right-only motion proposals are preserved for review; suppressing them would hide possible left-detector misses.

Neither a time-compatible right track nor a positive appearance patch verifies physical stone identity, a delivery, event start/end, or a settled board. The episode inventory deliberately leaves physical throw count and cross-view identity unknown. The prepared [blind review packet](blind-throw-review/README.md) could supply a stronger event reference when independently labelled, but no score or F1 is reported here. This recording also belongs to the 2024 split-screen production family; camera-family generalization remains untested.

## Episode-to-partial-board handoff

An [additive state handoff](corridor-right-episode-partial-states-development.json) uses the saved exact-source interval/full-match ID reconciliation to attach existing full-match partial boards to the 20 cross-view episodes. **Twelve** episodes have at least one stable camera-local stone observation, **three** have an accepted screen candidate but no stable board, and **five** have no accepted member (the end-reset group and four right-only proposals). The S12/S13 duplicate group retains the accepted S12 candidate's board status without counting S13 as another throw. All 20 episodes remain `simulation_ready: false` and `complete_game_state: false`.

This join makes post-candidate observations available in the same ordered episode inventory, including explicit missing outcomes. It does not certify that an episode was one physical delivery, that its observed stones are a complete settled board, or that adjacent observations form a verified state transition. The underlying metric positions are conditional projections; cross-view stone identities and physical release/settle times remain unknown.
