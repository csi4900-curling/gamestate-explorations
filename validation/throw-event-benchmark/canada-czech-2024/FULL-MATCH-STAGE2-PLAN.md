The stage-one [audit](full-match-stage1-audit-postscore.json) froze 116 corridor candidates from the entire 0–6683 s source; preserve that set without selection by score or source review.

This choice was made before any full-match high-resolution inference to limit transfer size. Verify full-source alignment at predeclared source seconds **500, 1500, 2500, 3500, 4500, 5500, 6300**, requiring zero best time adjustment at every checkpoint. Abort motion inference if the source or alignment check fails.

Run the existing fixed 10 s right-view anchor grid over the full high-resolution recording with source offset 0. Partition all 116 corridor candidates deterministically into shards of at most 8 and run the unchanged 0.5 s / 12 s receiving-motion scan, same-shot registration hold, 4.1 s gap selector, conditional cap-dropout rescue, frozen three-patch model and duplicate screen. Preserve every stage, all 116 candidate IDs, and all source hashes. Do not use displayed-end or official-score data to choose candidates, anchors, or paths.

The single-anchor full-match result will reveal whether one recording-local pose covers the changing right-camera views. If it fails, report the coverage and separately design a multi-anchor extension; do not hand-pick new anchors or silently substitute one during this run. The already frozen interval result remains unchanged. No full-match F1 is available without exhaustive independent labels; official six-end totals and display markers are context, not event truth.
