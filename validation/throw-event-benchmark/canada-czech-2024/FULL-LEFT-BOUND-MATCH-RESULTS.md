Registration, the original high-resolution source, 0.5 s sampling, colour proposal logic, and path selector are held fixed. The runner saves one hash-checked paired artifact per candidate. This is a complete check of the **left corridor subset**, not all raw candidates, views, or possible deliveries.

The **exact frozen patch model** (SHA-256 `10036a8b13e7e35f0be3cfd7280e9e0afaa3cf5b5da6cecc5623fb79695e0670`) supports all 12 isolated extended paths using the unchanged one-of-three rule: ten paths have at least two positive patches and two have one; the individual `*-extended-patch.json` artifacts sit beside the paired results. The [overhead-darkness development check](left-bound-full-match/changed-overhead-development.json) would abstain on none of these 12. Seven have all positive samples measurable; five have a top-edge positive sample where the 100-pixel overhead band is unavailable, but each has another measurable positive sample with low darkness. This preserves the observed paths under that proposed abstention, not under an adopted detector gate.

An [integrated postscore screen replay](left-bound-full-match/integrated-screen-development.json) first reproduces **all 116 frozen decisions exactly**, including all frozen timed-path duplicate pairs. It then replaces only these 12 motion selections and their three patch samples, reruns the unchanged appearance screen and global timed-path deduplicator, and outputs **91 provisional accepted candidates**: all 79 frozen acceptances plus these 12, no removals and no new shared-timed-path duplicate pairs. The closest of the new observed path windows and a frozen accepted path is separated by 38.0 s. This rules out overlap under the existing timed-path dedup criterion; it does not prove distinct physical throws or remove the two source-reviewed false paths still present among the 79.

The [partial-state handoff](LEFT-BOUND-STATE-HANDOFF.md) extends this development sequence through 12 new quiet-tail board observations, then interleaves them with the unchanged 79 frozen board rows. It preserves 147 raw candidates, 91 partial-board attempts and explicit unknowns for full game state and simulation. This materially strengthens the case that the original left proposal bound censored real stone observations beyond the S41 spot check. It does **not** establish 12 additional true throw events, a 91-throw count, event-level recall, precision, or F1. The bound and follow-up inspections were chosen after examining this match. The frozen 79-candidate result is unchanged. Cross-view/physical identity, near-player true-throw stress tests and exhaustive independent throw labels remain necessary before promotion into the reproducible detector.
