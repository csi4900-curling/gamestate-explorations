# Full Canada–Czechia cross-view candidate scan

This is a **prediction-only, full-source candidate inventory**, not a throw count or an F1 measurement. The [prerun plan](FULL-CORRIDOR-RIGHT-SCAN-PLAN.json) froze the 0–6683 s source, 116 left-corridor candidates, right-camera tracker, direction-aware temporal join, and episode grouping before the full right scan. It specified no appearance gate and no event decision. The source includes play, resets, and non-play footage; a 1 Hz scan can also miss activity around camera cuts.

The unchanged [right scan](full-corridor-right-probe-first-transfer.json) produced 379 raw tracks and **131 directional right proposals**. The [moving-tail derivative](full-corridor-right-tail-first-transfer.json) retained all 131, including 83 original short prefixes, 46 short sustained tails from longer tracks, and two tracks without a supported short tail. A temporal [left/right join](full-corridor-right-corrob-first-transfer.json) found **91 unique compatible right proposals**, **13 ambiguous left candidates**, and **12 left candidates without a compatible right proposal**. Compatibility here means only colour, travel direction, camera order, and a 20 s window. It does not verify the same physical stone.

The [episode inventory](full-corridor-right-episodes-first-transfer.json) preserves every one of the 116 left candidates and 131 right proposals across **133 episodes**: 91 one-to-one temporal candidates, 12 left-only, 23 right-only, five left candidates with multiple right proposals, and two groups in which left candidates share one right proposal. The right-only episodes are retained as possible missed-left review targets. The ambiguous episodes retain their competing associations rather than assigning a physical throw identity.

The [partial-state handoff](full-corridor-right-episode-partial-states-first-transfer.json) attaches existing full-match screening and board observations by exact candidate ID: **66 episodes have a partial board**, 13 have an accepted provisional candidate without a stable board, and 54 have no accepted left member. **Zero** episodes have a complete, simulation-ready game state. A partial board is a camera-local observation, not a verified settled state after a physical delivery.

The existing same-source provisional screen accepted 79 of the 116 left candidates and withheld 37. Of the accepted 79, 74 have a unique right match, three are ambiguous, and two have no right match. Of the withheld 37, **17 also have a unique right match**, ten are ambiguous, and ten have no right match. These are cross-checks against a prior model decision, not independent truth. They show that a unique temporal match cannot by itself be used as a hard accept/reject rule for deliveries.

## Right-only appearance diagnostic

A postscan [frozen-patch audit](full-corridor-right-only-patch-evidence-postscan.json) scored the first, middle, and last tracked boxes for all 23 right-only proposals. Fifteen had zero positive patches, four had one, two had two, and two had three. The two apparently strongest tracks were selected **after** scoring for a [source-frame contact sheet](full-corridor-right-only-top-patch-review/three-positive-right-only.jpg) with its [provenance manifest](full-corridor-right-only-top-patch-review/manifest.json). The [targeted source review](full-corridor-right-only-top-patch-review/source-review.json) finds that R01:A035 follows a player's head and R01:A100 follows a person's hat or clothing. Both are appearance false positives in the marked frames. This same-agent review is not an independent full-match reference and says nothing conclusive about the other 21 right-only proposals.

The result argues for preserving right-only detections as **reviewable hypotheses**, while requiring stronger temporal object identity and eventual independent labels before promoting them to throws. A high-recall candidate stage remains useful, but appearance, temporal agreement, and partial board presence have each shown failure modes on this source. No full-match precision, recall, F1, physical revolution count, release time, settle time, complete board, or simulation parameter is established here.

An unchanged [ring-plus-underpoint context diagnostic](FULL-RIGHT-CONTEXT-PAIR-RESULTS.md) now scores all 131 right proposals. It sends both reviewed person tracks to the cluttered tier and preserves every proposal, including 22 cluttered right-only tracks and one with insufficient samples. This is review prioritization, not an event filter or independent accuracy result.
