# Czech cross-resolution receiving-path consistency

The [reproducible overlap ledger](full-cross-resolution-receiving-path-overlap-development.json) compares the 1080p receiving-camera continuation attached to each left candidate with the separately generated 360p right-camera proposal prefix at **exactly shared source times**, dividing the full-frame 1080p pixel centers by three. The test covers all **119** time/colour/direction-compatible left/right candidate pairs in the Czech full-match inventory. **42 pairs** have at least two shared sampled times; their median centre distances range from **0.204 to 1.789 pixels at 360p**. The other **77** pairs have no exact sampled overlap, so this measurement says nothing about their path agreement.

An all-pairs comparison across all 116 left and 131 right proposals yields **15,196** possible pairings. Only **43** pairs share at least two exact sample times: the same 42 temporal links above and one nonlinked pair with a median centre separation of **129.785 pixels**. This single nonlinked example is an illustrative comparison, not a calibrated false-match distribution or a threshold justification.

Episode E022 shows both the value and the limit. The provisional S23 receiving path and right proposal R01:A029 overlap at 780, 781 and 782 s with a median separation of **0.963 px**; independent source-pixel inspection of A029's highlighted boxes shows a moving stone. S25 and S27 both overlap R01:A034 at three times with a median separation of **0.428 px**, but that source prefix follows a **person**. Thus close image paths can verify consistency between two processing branches while agreeing on the wrong object for a throw. S25 and S27 also share the same 1080p receiving path, so their agreement with A034 is not two independent confirmations.

The [episode-state handoff](full-source-diverse-episode-state-handoff-development.json) now carries these 119 measurements alongside the 131 right-path appearance records and 66 preserved partial boards. No path overlap is promoted to physical stone identity, a verified throw, or a simulation-ready state. The two pipelines process related pixels of the same broadcast, and a close center distance does not validate the metric homography or connect a stone across an unobserved interval.

Run `python prototype/scripts/measure_czech_cross_resolution_path_overlap.py` after moving the existing output aside. The ledger pins source, left-motion, right-tail and temporal-link hashes, and preserves each shared-time pixel pair. Unit tests cover the exact-time scaling and no-overlap abstention: `PYTHONPATH=prototype feasibility/scripts/python.sh -m pytest -q prototype/tests/test_cross_resolution_path_overlap.py`.
