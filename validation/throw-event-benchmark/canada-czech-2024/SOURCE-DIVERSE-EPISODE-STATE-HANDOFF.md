# Czech full-match appearance-to-state handoff

The [lossless episode-state handoff](full-source-diverse-episode-state-handoff-development.json) attaches the frozen 2024 right-view patch score, source-pixel review provenance and [cross-resolution path overlap](CROSS-RESOLUTION-PATH-OVERLAP.md) to the existing Czech cross-view [133-episode inventory](full-corridor-right-episodes-first-transfer.json) and [partial states](full-corridor-right-episode-partial-states-first-transfer.json). It preserves **all 116 left candidates, all 131 right proposals, all 119 temporal left/right pairs and all 66 observed partial boards**. No episode is promoted to a verified physical throw, complete game state or simulation-ready state.

The 59 reviewed right prefixes remain explicitly labelled as **21 moving-stone** and **38 person/equipment** source-pixel observations; 72 right prefixes have no such review. Among the 133 episodes, the handoff assigns lossless review priorities: 21 have at least one source-reviewed stone prefix, 72 have an unreviewed positive right patch, 23 are right-only with person/equipment prefixes, five have a left candidate but no sampled stone-like right prefix, and 12 have no right proposal. These priorities are workflow hints, not event decisions or measurements of precision and recall.

Episode E022 illustrates the association problem: four right tracks fit three left candidates in time. The sampled pixels show a moving stone on R01:A029 and people/equipment on R01:A030, A034 and A036, so A029 is a **source-pixel preference** for follow-up. S23 and A029 agree on three exact-time receiving-camera image centers within a median 0.963 px at 360p. S25 and S27 similarly agree with A034 within 0.428 px, but A034 follows a person. The handoff keeps every ID and `cross_view_identity_verified: false`. The 20 sampled positive one-to-one paths similarly remain stone-pixel observations, not verified cross-camera physical identities. The 66 partial boards retain their original camera-local coordinates, completeness flags and simulation blockers; the 13 accepted episodes without a stable board retain their explicit no-board status records.

The [reviewed-stone replay gap ledger](REVIEWED-STONE-REPLAY-GAPS.md) follows all 21 reviewed moving-stone prefixes into their motion and board observations. Only seven have a measured shared image path and a partial board, and even those seven lack a verified moving-stone-to-settled-stone link. This is the current concrete blocker to a truthful game-state replay.

Regenerate with `python prototype/scripts/export_czech_source_diverse_episode_handoff.py` after moving the existing output aside. The exporter checks source and input hashes, exact episode/state membership, all candidate and temporal-pair IDs, sampled-frame agreement between patch scores and source reviews, partial-board conservation, and false simulation-ready flags. Unit checks for dropped or duplicate candidates, orphan path pairs and mismatched review frames are in `prototype/tests/test_source_diverse_episode_handoff.py` (`PYTHONPATH=prototype feasibility/scripts/python.sh -m pytest -q prototype/tests/test_source_diverse_episode_handoff.py`). The next stages still require physical event truth, reliable cross-camera stone identity, settled-board coverage and metric-position validation before a game replay can be called coaching-grade.
