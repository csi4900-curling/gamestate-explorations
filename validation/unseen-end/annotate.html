What do I actually label?
  1. Watch one sheet throughout. A delivery is a player sending a stone down that sheet. Repositioning stones or clearing the ice is different; flag ambiguity.
  2. Mark a start just before the delivery, then a finish after the stone leaves the departure view. These are broad brackets, not release-time measurements.
  3. Follow the receiving view. When visible motion stops, watch a few more seconds and capture a clear board. Click the middle of each visible stone body using its handle colour.
  4. Include stones in front of the rings. Leave completeness unchecked if people, framing or missing views prevent accounting for every in-play stone.
  5. Use the uncertainty field whenever you would otherwise guess. You do not need to judge strategy, score or legality.
