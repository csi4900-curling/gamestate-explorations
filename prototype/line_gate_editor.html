Configure a stone-crossing line

Open a local video, seek to a clear view, pause, then choose two endpoints on the image. This sets a fixed image line; it does not learn rink geometry or follow camera movement. The crossing point is the centre of a detected stone box. Video stays on your machine.

No video selected.

Endpoint order defines the sides. For a horizontal line drawn left to right, positive is below the line and negative is above it. Only crossings between the endpoints count.

The supplied ByteTrack configuration uses its upstream thresholds; 0.1 retains its low-confidence association inputs. These settings are explicit defaults, not measured accuracy probabilities.
